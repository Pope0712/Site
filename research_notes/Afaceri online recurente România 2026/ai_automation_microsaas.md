## 1. Productized AI services on monthly retainers (AI chatbots, voice receptionists, WhatsApp, lead follow-up, n8n/Make/Zapier agencies): prices, clients needed, real cases vs hype, churn

### Takeaway
Internationally, the done-for-you AI receptionist/automation offer is usually priced at about $1-3k setup plus $300-800/month. At that price, 4-10 clients would reach 2-3k EUR MRR. In Romania, the same service sells for 40-300 EUR/month, so 10-60 clients would be needed. The numbers behind the "AI automation agency" model are almost all vendor or playbook claims. No independent income distribution exists. Generalist offers sold through cold outreach are described as saturated. Churn clusters in the first 90 days, and pass-through costs (voice minutes, WhatsApp, LLM) eat into retainers.

### Cited Findings
**International price points (setup + monthly)**
- Off-the-shelf AI receptionist software costs end customers about $25-300/month, with most full-featured plans at $50-200 — [OnceHub, 2026 cost guide](https://www.oncehub.com/blog/answering-service-cost). Another guide puts most small-business plans at $39-99/month — [TRTC](https://trtc.io/blog/details/ai-receptionist-cost-pricing-guide).
- These plans are usually tied to included calls or minutes, and overage charges can push the real bill well above the headline fee. Bespoke voice-agent implementations cost about £2,000-4,000+ — [Fasthosts (updated 9 Jul 2026)](https://www.fasthosts.co.uk/blog/how-much-does-an-ai-receptionist-cost/).
- Agency tier (vendor blog): starter agents cost about $3,000 setup with $300-1,500/month retainers. An off-hours receptionist costs $2,000-5,000 setup plus usage-based monthly billing — [Buldrr](https://buldrr.com/7-ai-agents-businesses-are-paying-for-in-2026/). This is vendor marketing.
- An Indie Hackers playbook post sells an AI receptionist with booking as a done-for-you service. Its stack is Callin.io + n8n + Cal.com, aimed at local service businesses with $500K-$5M revenue. Pricing is "$1-2K setup + $300-800 MRR per client", at about 80% margin, with an estimated 2-3 weeks to first revenue. Dental practices are listed as a first segment. This is a how-to outline, not a verified revenue report — [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/building-a-profitable-ai-voice-saas-agency-300-800-mrr-per-client-frAbgO1yQMfHOFFtY3gE).
- Other playbook pages in the same search gave conflicting dental-office figures: $500-1,500 setup in one, $3,000-4,000 in another, and $700-1,000/month. All come from vendors selling voice platforms or playbooks (e.g., [Trillet](https://trillet.ai/blogs/how-much-money-can-you-make-with-ai-voice-agency), [Autocalls](https://autocalls.ai/article/how-to-start-ai-calling-agency)). Which page gave which number could not be verified.
- Fiverr/Upwork gig pricing (SEO blog, no methodology): junior automation gigs at $80-350 per workflow, AI chatbot builds for small businesses at $500-2,000, and a single-workflow agent at $1,500-2,500 plus $400-800/month maintenance retainers. The same blog says generic "ChatGPT writes your blog post for $5" gigs have collapsed, and that buyers now want productized fixed-price offers — [betonai.net, June 2026](https://betonai.net/the-10-highest-earning-ai-automation-gigs-on-fiverr-upwork-in-june-2026-what-is-actually-selling-real-earnings-data-and-the-gig-templates-that-convert/).

All-in voice agents cost about $0.07-0.30/min in 2026, depending on LLM and voice — [Ainora](https://ainora.lt/blog/ai-voice-agent-cost-per-minute-2026), [Techsy](https://techsy.io/en/blog/ai-voice-agent-pricing), [Speechify comparison](https://speechify.ai/blog/best-voice-agent-platforms-2026). At 10,000 min/month, one comparison found Retell at about $1,144 and Vapi at $819-1,290 (same cluster; the sources are partly affiliates).

**Romanian price points for AI chatbots and automation**

The other puts a simple FAQ bot at 2,000-5,000 EUR, 8,000-15,000 EUR with integrations, and 20,000-50,000+ EUR for enterprise, with 200-5,000 EUR/month maintenance. - **DentAIM** (WhatsApp reminders and follow-up for dental offices): Starter is 250 RON first month (setup included), then 200 RON/month. Pro is 450 RON, then 300 RON/month, both excluding VAT — [dentaim.ro](https://dentaim.ro/).

### Inferences
- **Client math for 2,500 EUR MRR** (approximate, at roughly 5 RON/EUR; that rate is not from the sources):
  - International done-for-you receptionist at $300-800/month: about 4-9 clients, plus setup fees of $1-3k each that cover acquisition.
  - Romanian packaged dental WhatsApp tools at 200-300 RON/month (DentAIM): about 40-60 clinics. At Vastoma-style 99-299 EUR/month: about 9-25.
  - Romanian agency chatbot at 300-800 RON/month: about 16-40 clients.
- **Productized SaaS competitors set the Romanian price ceiling.** In Romania, productized SaaS competitors (AllAI 24-59 EUR, CatyAI 29 EUR, DentAIM about 40-60 EUR) already anchor prices low. A solo service operator charging 150-300 EUR/month must offer real integration work (calendar/PMS, follow-up flows, reporting), not just a bot.
- **Pass-through costs need explicit pricing.** Voice receptionists carry real per-minute costs ($0.07-0.30/min). A clinic using 1,000 minutes/month would cost $70-300 in usage alone, so retainers need usage tiers or overage pass-through.
- **What is actually working (pattern across sources):** a narrow vertical, a fixed-price productized offer, a visible result quickly, and annual prepayment. Generic "AI automation agency" positioning sold by cold email is the most-cited saturated segment.

### Gaps
- No independent dataset of AI-agency owner incomes or of retainer churn was found. All churn figures are vendor or playbook claims.
- No verified Romanian case study (named client count or MRR) of a solo AI-automation operator was found.
- Romanian-language voice quality on Vapi/Retell/ElevenLabs stacks was not verified.
- The Upwork "2026 Freelance Economy Report" figures could not be confirmed.

## 2. Micro-SaaS for solo founders in 2025-2026: which niches work, time to 1k/3k MRR, distribution channels, benchmark data, AI-wrapper risk

### Takeaway
Verified-revenue datasets show the typical indie SaaS earns very little. The median is about $145-186 MRR, about 23.5% exceed $1k MRR, about 6% exceed $10k, and median month-over-month growth is 0%. Chrome extensions are worse: 57% earn nothing and 6.3% clear $1k/month. Reaching 2-3k EUR MRR solo puts a founder roughly in the top quintile and typically takes 12-18+ months. Thin AI wrappers have B2C-like retention: ChartMogul measured median GRR of 40% for AI-native companies.

### Cited Findings
**Revenue distribution (verified or tracked datasets)**
- MicroConf State of Independent SaaS 2024: 17.7% of revenue-generating founders are under $1,000 MRR, the single largest group. The data was collected in late 2023 from founders already generating revenue. This is older data — [MicroConf](https://microconf.com/saas-this-year). Just under 700 usable responses — [Startups For the Rest of Us ep. 721](https://www.startupsfortherestofus.com/episodes/episode-721-7-key-takeaways-from-the-2024-state-of-independent-saas-report).
- An analysis of 3,787 bootstrapped SaaS found a median MRR of $168 for the 2025 cohort, 23.5% over $1k MRR, and median MoM growth of 0%. It notes survivorship bias, since dead projects stop being tracked — [BigIdeasDB, State of Indie SaaS Revenue 2026](https://bigideasdb.com/state-of-indie-saas-revenue-2026).
- TrustMRR (Stripe/Paddle-verified) derived stats: median about $145, average about $4,300-4,682. The page is internally inconsistent and also states about $7 MRR median for some subsets. Across 748 AI startups under $5k MRR, the median is $177 — [BigIdeasDB TrustMRR page](https://bigideasdb.com/trustmrr). Another pull of 5,596 TrustMRR startups found a median of $186/month — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/rudratosh/i-pulled-verified-revenue-for-5596-startups-heres-exactly-what-to-build-in-2026-11-benchmarks-4eij). The top of the leaderboard exceeds $3.5M verified MRR (Sept 2026) — [ExitBid review](https://exitbid.io/blog/trustmrr-review-2026).
- One 2026 statistics roundup says the median revenue-generating micro-SaaS earns about $145 MRR and only 6.1% clear $10k MRR. It cites a RockingWeb analysis of 1,000+ micro-SaaS suggesting founders should plan for 12-18 months to reach "meaningful revenue", with thin sourcing — [mean.ceo](https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=11171).
- ChartMogul data cited by MicroConf: the average SaaS takes 2 years 9 months to hit $1M ARR — [MicroConf stage pages](https://microconf.com/founders/0-10k-arr).

**Channel-specific data**
- **Chrome extensions:** of 112 revenue-verified extensions, 57.1% earn nothing, 6.3% clear $1,000/month, and the average is about $295/month (Sept 2026). The publisher sells research data — [BigIdeasDB](https://bigideasdb.com/how-to-monetize-a-chrome-extension). The Chrome Web Store has no built-in payments, so developers handle billing and licensing themselves — [Fungies](https://fungies.io/?p=39126). Flippa listings show small extensions with seller-reported profits of about $350-700/month asking $10-21k, e.g., 17.4k users and $7.3k yearly profit — [Flippa](https://flippa.com/12839069).
- **Shopify apps:** developers keep 100% of their first $1M in gross app revenue earned from 1 Jan 2025, and 85% above that. Older docs show a different 0%/15%/20% structure, so check the live page — [Shopify.dev revenue share](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/revenue-share). Shopify apps listed for sale have a median trailing revenue of $85k, but on only n=29 listings, which are not representative of all developers — [BigIdeasDB](https://bigideasdb.com/how-to-sell-a-shopify-app).
- **WordPress plugins:** Freemius says most AI plugins now sit on LLM APIs, which drove more monthly subscriptions and usage/credit-based pricing. In 2025 Freemius expanded beyond WordPress into micro-SaaS. No revenue numbers were published — [Freemius Year in Review 2025](https://freemius.com/year-in-review-2025/).
- **Acquisition channels:** 50% of bootstrapped SaaS founders lean on communities and referrals as their main channels. 57% of those running paid ads either wait 7+ months for ROI or cannot tell whether ads work. About half of independent SaaS products are solo-founded. Usage-based and hybrid pricing outperformed flat subscriptions, and short or reverse trials converted better than permanent free tiers — [Freemius State of Micro-SaaS 2025](https://freemius.com/blog/state-of-micro-saas-2025/), which builds on MicroConf survey data.

**Retention and churn benchmarks**
- ChartMogul's "AI churn wave" covered about 2,700 B2B SaaS, about 600 B2C and about 200 AI-native companies with at least $250k ARR, through 2025. AI-native companies had a median GRR of 40% and NRR of 48%, against B2C NRR of 49% and B2B SaaS NRR of 82%. AI-native GRR improved from 27% (Jan) to 40% (Sep 2025) — [ChartMogul report](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-retention-the-ai-churn-wave/), [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/the-ai-churn-wave). A different 2025 benchmark contradicts this, reporting a median NRR of 132% for AI-native companies — [The SaaS Barometer](https://thesaasbarometer.substack.com/p/beyond-benchmarks-2025). The difference is likely due to the samples.
- ChartMogul rule of thumb: with ARPA under $100, aim for monthly churn below 3.5%; with ARPA over $100, below 2.5%. This is guidance, not a measured benchmark — [ChartMogul](https://chartmogul.com/lp/saas-churn).
- SaaS Capital's 2025 retention study covers 1,000+ private B2B SaaS, but its ACV chart excludes companies under $1M ARR. Example: the $25-50K ACV band has a median NRR of 102% — [SaaS Capital](https://www.saas-capital.com/research/saas-retention-benchmarks-for-private-b2b-companies/). Bootstrapped companies grew a median 23% vs 25% for equity-backed in its 2025 survey (secondary summary) — [mean.ceo](https://blog.mean.ceo/?p=5720). Aleph x Benchmarkit CY-2025 shows median NRR of 102% and GRR of 84% — [Techsy NL](https://techsy.io/nl/blog/saas-metrics-die-ertoe-doen).

**Exit values (for context on what an MRR stream is worth)**
- Acquire.com moved to profit-based multiples by Jan 2024. The average confirmed SaaS multiple was 4.3x TTM profit, ranging 0.63-34x. This is 2024 data — [Livmo summary](https://livmo.com/blog/acquire-com-biannual-acquisition-multiples-report-feb-2023-livmo-summary/).
- A 2026 blog analysis of 600+ live listings finds the smallest deals average about 2.6x TTM revenue and 10.7x TTM profit, with sub-$1M ARR businesses at 2.5-4x revenue. This conflicts with the Livmo figure and is unverified — [Beancount blog, 11 Jul 2026](https://beancount.io/de/blog/2026/07/11/bootstrapped-saas-valuation-multiples-2026-acquire-com-indie-founders-guide).

**AI-wrapper commoditization**
- Jasper is the most-cited example of a thin wrapper hurt by ChatGPT improvements. Its revenue reportedly fell from about $120M (2023) to about $35-55M (2024), with sources differing — [Tian Pan, Apr 2026](https://tianpan.co/blog/2026/04/12/the-ai-wrapper-trap-when-your-moat-is-someone-elses-api-call).
- A Google VP warned in Feb 2026 that thin LLM wrappers and AI aggregators may not survive — [TechCrunch](https://www.techcrunch.com/2026/02/21/google-vp-warns-that-two-types-of-ai-startups-may-not-survive).
- Commonly circulated figures were found only in unsourced blogs and are not reliable: 65% churn within 90 days, about 3% wrapper survival, and about 3,800 AI startup shutdowns (same search cluster, e.g., [HatchWorks](https://hatchworks.com/blog/gen-ai/ai-wrapper-product-strategy/)).
- Budget LLM prices in 2026 are very low but inconsistently reported. GPT-5 nano is listed at about $0.05/M input tokens, Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite at $0.10/$0.40, and DeepSeek-V4 at $0.14/$0.28 (July 2026) — [Fungies](https://fungies.io/llm-api-pricing-comparison-2026-openai-claude-gemini-deepseek/), [CostBench](https://costbench.com/best/cheapest-llm-api/), [BenchLM](https://www.benchlm.ai/blog/posts/llm-pricing-2026). Model names and prices vary between these sources, so verify on the provider pages.

### Inferences
- **2-3k EUR MRR is roughly top-quintile.** With about 23.5% of tracked indie SaaS above $1k MRR and about 6% above $10k, a solo micro-SaaS at 2-3k EUR MRR sits in roughly the top 15-20% of tracked products, and that is among founders who already track revenue. This is a low-probability single bet, best treated as a portfolio item or a 12-24 month project.
- **Customer counts and churn replacement.** At an ARPA of about 25 EUR, roughly 100 paying customers are needed for 2.5k EUR. With 3-5% monthly churn, 3-5 new customers per month are needed just to stay flat.
- **Distribution is the binding constraint.** Marketplaces (Shopify App Store, WordPress.org, Chrome Web Store) provide built-in discovery, which suits a solo founder who cannot run paid ads profitably (57% cannot tell or wait 7+ months).
- **Wrapper risk is about moat, not cost.** Low LLM prices cut costs but also lower barriers for competitors. Durable small products combine a vertical workflow, integrations (e.g., ANAF/SPV, eMAG, SmartBill) and proprietary context rather than generic prompting.

### Gaps
- The MicroConf 2025/2026 State of Independent SaaS report itself, with churn and time-to-milestone data, could not be retrieved.
- No reliable "time to $1k MRR" distribution was found, only the unsourced 12-18 month guidance.
- No hard data on WordPress plugin revenue distribution (Freemius publishes no numbers).
- No primary Starter Story, Indie Hackers or Baremetrics dataset was retrieved.

## 3. Romanian compliance-driven demand (RO e-Factura, e-Transport, SAF-T/D406, RO e-TVA, 2026 changes): opportunities for small integration and automation tools, existing players and gaps

### Takeaway
The compliance stack is now fully mandatory for companies (e-Factura B2B and B2C, D406 for all taxpayer sizes). The cheap incumbents (Oblio about 29 EUR/year, FGO 15 RON/month, SmartBill from about 6 EUR/month, e-Factura sending free) cover basic invoicing well. 2026 actually reduced some pressure: the e-Factura obligation for CNP-identified individuals was dropped by Law 88/2026, and the RO e-TVA compliance-notification reply obligation ended. New Shopify/WooCommerce e-Factura apps appeared in 2026 at $0-30/month. The remaining openings are narrow integration and maintenance niches, such as eMAG↔invoicing↔WooCommerce glue, error and rejection monitoring, and multi-company SPV download/reconciliation for accountants. A solo operator is better placed to sell these as setup-plus-care services than as a mass-market SaaS.

### Cited Findings
**Regulatory status (2025-2026)**
- e-Factura became mandatory B2C from 1 Jan 2025. Fiscal receipts (bonuri fiscale) are not sent through e-Factura, while simplified invoices are — [Fiscalitatea](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/e-factura-obligatorie-si-in-relatia-b2c-incepand-cu-1-ianuarie-2025-23472/), [Fiscalitatea 2025 changes](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/e-factura-si-e-transport-in-2025-principalele-modificari-intrate-in-vigoare-de-anul-acesta-23885/).
- From 1 Jan 2026, the e-Factura transmission deadline is 5 **working** days from invoice issue (OUG 89/2025) — [ZarinaCRM guide](https://www.zarinacrm.ro/e-factura-ghid-complet/).
- **Individuals with CNP:**
  - In April 2026, coverage said e-Factura would become mandatory from 1 June 2026 for individuals with income from copyright or independent activities (OG 6/2026, Order 378/2026, Form 082 by 26 May) — [Capital.ro](https://www.capital.ro/e-factura-devine-obligatoriu-pentru-romanii-cu-venituri-din-drepturi-de-autor-sau-activitati-independente-se-aplica-de-la-1-iunie-2026.html).
  - That obligation was then **eliminated** by Law 88/2026 (MO 459, 29 May 2026), so use stays optional for CNP-identified individuals. Companies, PFAs/IIs with a CIF and associations remain covered — [certSIGN](https://www.certsign.ro/en/e-factura-2026-cine-sunt-persoanele-vizate-care-obtin-venituri-din-drepturi-de-autor/), [ContApp (updated 4 Jun 2026)](https://contapp.ro/blog/e-factura-pe-cnp/). Another source cites a law in MO 308/2026, a conflicting reference to verify in Monitorul Oficial — [StartupCafe](https://startupcafe.ro/ro-e-factura-extins-persoane-fizice-activitati-economice-continuitate-cnp-fotografi-influenceri-prestatori-amenzi-anaf-93635).
- **RO e-TVA:**
  - Earlier rules: penalties were deferred to 1 Jan 2026, after which ignoring a "Notificare de conformare" within 20 days risked fines of 1,000-10,000 lei (OG 22/2025) — [StartupCafe](https://startupcafe.ro/notificare-conformare-etva-clarificari-anaf-htm-28609).
  - Under OUG 89/2025, the mandatory-reply and sanction provisions ceased from 1 Jan 2026 — [Permis de Antreprenor](https://permisdeantreprenor.ro/notificare-conformare-ro-e-tva-2026/).
  - AvocatNet (11 Mar 2026) says the notification itself was eliminated, but the pre-filled VAT return (P300) is still provided — [AvocatNet](https://avocatnet.ro/t17671/declaratii-fiscale-precompletate.html).
- **SAF-T (D406):**

- **The plausible gaps are in glue and care work:**
  - Multi-channel order→invoice flows, e.g., WooCommerce + eMAG + courier AWB → SmartBill/Oblio, where Base.com dominates and custom connectors cost $249+.
  - Rejected-invoice and SPV-message monitoring with alerts, plus the certificate/token renewal hassle.
  - Bulk download of received e-invoices across many companies for accounting firms.
  - Matching e-Factura data against bank statements and the P300 pre-filled VAT return.
  These are better sold as setup (300-1,000 EUR) plus monitoring/maintenance retainer (20-100 EUR/month) to e-shops and accounting firms. That last price range is the author's estimate, not sourced.

- **Signals to watch.** ANAF's volume numbers (hundreds of millions of B2C e-invoices, millions of D406 filings) show the infrastructure is entrenched. Its enforcement focus (most fines related to e-Factura) means "zero rejected invoices" monitoring has a concrete selling point.

## 4. Romanian SME market: size, digitalization level, willingness to pay, typical spend on websites/marketing/automation, AI chatbot adoption, local competitors

### Takeaway
Romania has about 1.07M active firms, and SMEs make up about 99% of them. It is at or near the bottom of the EU on every digital metric: 44% of SMEs have basic digital intensity (EU 71%), and only 5.2% of firms with 10+ employees used AI in 2025 (EU about 20%), the lowest in the EU. That low base means a large unserved pool, but also low willingness to pay. Local AI chatbot products already sell at 24-300 EUR/month, and vendors report SME resistance to subscriptions.

## 6. Main risks: platform dependence, LLM API costs, client churn, liability (GDPR for chatbots handling patient data, EU AI Act transparency from Aug 2026)

### Takeaway
The chatbot transparency duty (AI Act Art. 50) has applied since 2 Aug 2026 and was not postponed by the Digital Omnibus, which delayed only high-risk rules. A clinic chatbot that asks for a reason for visit processes Article 9 health data, which requires a DPA and probably a DPIA. Meta has banned general-purpose AI bots on the WhatsApp Business API since 15 Jan 2026, though business-specific bots remain allowed. Voice and LLM usage costs, and B2C-like churn for AI-native products, are the main economic risks.

### Cited Findings
**EU AI Act**
- The Digital Omnibus, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 in force since 27 Jul 2026, pushed Annex III high-risk obligations to 2 Dec 2027 and Annex I to 2 Aug 2028. Article 50 transparency was left untouched: chatbot disclosure, deepfake labels and machine-readable marks have been enforceable since 2 Aug 2026 — [Dastra](https://www.dastra.eu/en/blog/ai-act-transparency-rules-what-changes-august-2-2026/60164), [ComplianceHub](https://compliancehub.wiki/eu-ai-act-article-50-transparency-digital-omnibus-2026/), [Pasquale Pillitteri](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/5190/digital-omnibus-ai-act-eu-parliament).
- Sources conflict on whether generative systems already on the market before 2 Aug 2026 got a marking deadline of 2 Dec 2026 — [ElevateConsult](https://elevateconsult.com/insights/eu-ai-act-article-50-icp-impact/), [Product Hunt discussion](https://www.producthunt.com/p/murror/the-eu-delayed-the-ai-act-rule-that-doesn-t-apply-to-you-the-one-that-does-went-live-in-august).
- Fines for Article 50 breaches can reach EUR 15M or 3% of worldwide turnover — [Scytale](https://scytale.ai/resources/eu-ai-act-updates/). This comes from law-firm and vendor blogs.
- The Commission's Art. 50 FAQ says people must be told they are dealing with AI "unless this is obvious", and the exception is read restrictively. The duty sits mainly with the provider, while GDPR transparency sits with the clinic — summarized in [LearnSignal (Ireland health guide)](https://www.learnsignal.com/blog/ai-chatbots-patient-communication-gdpr-transparency-health-services-ireland-guide/).

**GDPR for clinic and health bots**
- Roles: the practice is usually the controller and the AI vendor/operator the processor. A signed Art. 28 DPA is needed before health data reaches the vendor, plus equivalent contracts with each sub-processor (LLM, telephony) — [LearnSignal](https://www.learnsignal.com/blog/ai-chatbots-patient-communication-gdpr-transparency-health-services-ireland-guide/), [Layer3 Labs (Aug 2026)](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/gdpr-ai-health-data-compliance).
- A booking flow that asks for a reason for visit processes Art. 9 data. One clinic policy uses Art. 6(1)(b) for booking, 6(1)(a) consent for call recording, and Art. 9(2)(h) for volunteered health info — [TwojeZnamiona policy](https://twojeznamiona.pl/en/polityka/).
- A DPIA is "often required" or "practically mandatory" for AI on health data according to these vendor guides. Strictly, Art. 35 is a case-by-case high-risk test — [Layer3 Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/gdpr-ai-health-data-compliance).
- Design tip: keep the assistant from knowing which patients are registered, and handle only general info and anonymized availability — [Crox dental AI guide](https://crox.io/insights/ai-for-dental-practices).

**Tax and regulatory context for the solo operator's own company**
- From 1 Jan 2026 the microenterprise regime has a single 1% tax on revenue and a cap of 100,000 EUR, with the 3% rate removed (OUG 89/2025). The cap is tested on combined revenue with linked enterprises, and PFA revenue counts if the PFA holder owns more than 25% of an SRL — [Fiscalitatea](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/microintreprindere-in-2026-2-schimbari-importante-pentru-firme-24602/), [Contabilul Manager](https://contabilul.manager.ro/a/29820/impozit-micro-2026-ghid-practic-conditii-cota-unica-si-ce-obligatii-trebuie-respectate.html).
- 2-3k EUR MRR (24-36k EUR/year) sits well below the 100k EUR cap.

### Inferences
- **Compliance work is part of the clinic product.** A solo operator selling to clinics needs:
  - a template DPA,
  - a sub-processor list (LLM, voice, telephony, WhatsApp BSP),
  - an AI disclosure message,
  - a short DPIA template,
  - EU data residency or zero-retention settings where possible.
  This work can itself be productized as part of a "GDPR-ready clinic bot" offer and is a differentiator against cheap generic tools.
- **WhatsApp bots must stay narrow.** The Meta policy means WhatsApp automation must stay business-specific (bookings, reminders, support), not an open-ended assistant.
- **Price usage separately.** Usage-based pass-through (minutes or messages) protects margin against heavy-use clients and LLM price swings.
- **Diversify the base.** A base split across 10-30 small clients is more resilient to single-client churn than 3-4 big retainers, but it raises support load. That trade-off should be weighed against the 25% first-quarter churn pattern reported by playbooks.
