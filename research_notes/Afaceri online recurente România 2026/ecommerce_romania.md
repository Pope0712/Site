# E-commerce models accessible from Romania for a solo operator (target 2,000-3,000 EUR/month net), state as of Oct 2026

Method note for the report writer: research date 2026-10-20. Full-page fetches were blocked in this environment (DNS/proxy refusals for forbes.ro, wall-street.ro, gpec.ro, marketplace.emag.ro, consilium.europa.eu, marqetir.com, comunicatedepresa.ro). Almost every figure below therefore comes from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not from a full read of each page. Treat single-source numbers as indicative. Exchange rate used for conversions: about 5.24 lei/EUR (ZF site header, Oct 2026, [ZF](https://www.zf.ro/business-international/cea-mai-mare-platforma-de-comert-electronic-din-polonia-vrea-sa-23160756)).

## 1. Romanian e-commerce market 2024-2026: size, growth, cash on delivery, returns, AOV, couriers, lockers

### Takeaway
Romanian online retail of physical goods is about 8-8.5 bn EUR in 2025, growing at single digits (6-12% depending on the source). Growth now comes from higher prices rather than more orders. Cash on delivery (ramburs) is still about 60-65% of orders and drives higher returns and refusals. Logistics is concentrating fast: Sameday (eMAG group) bought Cargus in 2026, and easybox lockers carry a large and growing share of parcels.

### Cited Findings
**Market size and growth (the sources disagree because they define the market differently)**
- A GPeC + ARMO report (via secondary coverage) puts the market above 8.5 bn EUR in 2025, about +12% YoY — [Mobilissimo](https://www.mobilissimo.ro/stiri-diverse/ce-vor-romanii-sa-cumpere-online-segmentul-e-commerce-in-crestere-in-2025)
- Gomag "eCommerce Pulse 2026": physical-goods e-commerce above 8 bn EUR in 2025 (+7-8%). It projects above 8.7 bn EUR in 2026 and about 13 bn EUR by 2032, a CAGR of about 7.25% — [Forbes România](https://forbes.ro/piata-de-ecommerce-din-romania-depaseste-8-miliarde-de-euro-in-2025-si-se-indreapta-spre-13-miliarde-pana-in-2032-492528); [Bursa](https://www.bursa.ro/raport-piata-de-ecommerce-din-romania-a-depasit-8-miliarde-de-euro-in-2025-68566858)
- MerchantPro: 8.1 bn EUR in 2025, about +6%, with **no increase in the number of transactions**. Coverage says "Romanians buy more expensively, not more often", and Temu and Trendyol are pressuring local merchants — [Știri de Cluj](https://www.stiridecluj.ro/economic/romanii-cumpara-mai-scump-nu-mai-des-adevarul-din-spatele-cresterii-de-6-a-comertului-electronic-din-romania-si-ce-platforme-domina-piata-in-2026)
- Ecommerce Europe / EuroCommerce European E-Commerce Report 2026 (broader B2C scope, includes services): 12.9 bn EUR in 2025 (+10%) and an estimated 13.5 bn EUR in 2026 — [Wall-Street.ro](https://www.wall-street.ro/articol/ecommerce/analiza-romania-cel-mai-mare-sector-de-e-commerce-din-europa-de-est)
- GPeC figure cited at a summit (search extract, broad scope): about 11.7 bn EUR in 2024 and about 12.8 bn EUR projected for 2025 — [GPeC blog](https://www.gpec.ro/blog/gpec-summit-mai-2025)
- eMAG's Tudor Manea (Black Friday 2025): e-commerce to reach 3.5% of GDP in 2025 — [Capital](https://www.capital.ro/black-friday-la-emag-noutatile-din-acest-an-printre-22-milioane-de-oferte-tudor-manea-segmentul-e-commerce-va-ajunge-in-2025-la-35-din-pib.html)

**Average order value (AOV)**
- Gomag merchant data: AOV of 452 lei in 2025, down from 494 lei in 2024 (about 86 EUR). A GPeC summit recap instead says AOV grew by a single digit in 2025. The two datasets cover different merchants — [ReturnLab citing Gomag](https://returnlab.io/blog/retururi-romania-2026-ghid-complet); [GPeC blog](https://www.gpec.ro/blog/gpec-summit-14oct)

**Cash on delivery and returns**
- About two-thirds of online orders are still paid by ramburs. A COD-payment operator reports an average acceptance rate of 85% (internal, unaudited data) — [Forbes România](https://www.forbes.ro/?p=518750)
- COD is 60-65% of orders nationally (Gomag data). Stores that push online payment have higher AOV and fewer returns — [ReturnLab](https://returnlab.io/blog/retururi-romania-2026-ghid-complet)
- NETOPIA at a GPeC event: return rate of 3-5% for card-paid orders vs 11-12% for COD orders. This is a speaker statement, not a study — [ReturnLab](https://returnlab.io/blog/rata-de-retur-cum-o-reduci)
- Romania has no official national return rate. The commonly cited "about 3%" is a courier per-parcel figure, not a store-level rate — [ReturnLab](https://returnlab.io/blog/retururi-romania-2026-ghid-complet)
- Agency guide: COD refusal at the door can reach 10-25% in some niches (a vendor claim; another source says 10-30%) — [MM Creative](https://mmcreative.ro/sisteme-plata-magazine-online-romania/)
- Shipo (courier aggregator) via Ziare: one refused parcel wipes out the effort of about 3 completed orders. Its example store has 300 orders/month and a 5% refusal rate, loses about 750 lei/month, and that equals 12.5% of the store's net profit — [Ziare.com](https://ziare.com/e-commerce/gaura-neagra-a-e-commerce-ului-peste-12-la-suta-din-profitul-magazinelor-online-dispare-pe-coletele-refuzate-2003830)

**Couriers and per-parcel costs**
- The top 10 courier operators had about 5.84 bn lei combined turnover in 2025, about +15% — [Economedia](https://economedia.ro/analiza-topul-companiilor-de-curierat-din-romania-2025.html)
- ANCOM parcel-volume shares: Sameday 32%, FAN Courier 26%, Cargus 11%. Average operator revenue per parcel is about 15 lei, or 12.5 lei for domestic parcels — [Playtech](https://playtech.ro/2026/apare-un-nou-gigant-pe-piata-de-curierat-din-romania-cargus-a-fost-deja-preluat/)
- Sameday: 2025 revenue of 1.6 bn lei and 135 million parcels delivered. easybox deliveries rose about 30% in 2025 — [The Diplomat](https://www.thediplomat.ro/2026/06/03/sameday-ends-2025-with-1-6-billion-ron-in-revenue-and-135-million-parcels-delivered/)
- Sameday invested about 80 M EUR in 2025, and easybox capacity in Romania grew by more than 20% — [The Diplomat](https://www.thediplomat.ro/2025/10/29/sameday-invests-80-million-euros-in-technology-regional-expansion-and-the-growth-of-the-easybox-network-in-2025/)
- Older Sameday data (likely pre-2025): about 47% of Sameday domestic parcels (over 27 M) went to easybox, and 73% of those were prepaid online — [Economedia](https://economedia.ro/black-friday-dupa-investitii-de-peste-80-de-milioane-de-euro-in-ultimul-an-ompania-de-curierat-sameday-este-pregatita-sa-proceseze-1-milion-de-colete-pe-zi.html)
- Sameday-Cargus: the Competition Council approved the deal on 2 Apr 2026. Conditions: keep Cargus contracts unchanged for 12 months and divest the Cargus locker network to an independent operator — [Sameday](https://sameday.ro/news/romanias-competition-council-approves-the-takeover-of-cargus-by-sameday/?lang=en). Closing: Sameday has held 100% of Cargus since 3 Aug 2026 — [Știripesurse](https://www.stiripesurse.ro/sameday-a-finalizat-achizitia-cargus-cele-doua-companii-incep-integrarea-retelelor-de-livrare-si-a-echipelor_3909253)
- Locker counts conflict: more than 6,000 easybox lockers in Romania (June 2026) — [Business24](https://business24.ro/logistica-urbana/extindere-retea-lockere-peste-sase-mii-1656308); vs about 8,000 easybox across RO/HU/BG — [Știripesurse](https://www.stiripesurse.ro/sameday-a-finalizat-achizitia-cargus-cele-doua-companii-incep-integrarea-retelelor-de-livrare-si-a-echipelor_3909253)
- Small-merchant rates via the Shipo aggregator (date unclear, indicative): Sameday easybox-to-easybox at 12.99 lei for 1-2 kg and easybox-to-address at 16.99 lei. The guide and homepage show easybox "from 9.99 lei + VAT" / "from 10.99 lei". Calculator prices were promotional until 31 Jul 2026 and exclude the fuel index and extra services — [Shipo offer](https://shipo.ro/commercial-offer); [Shipo tarife](https://shipo.ro/tarife-curierat); [Shipo easybox guide](https://shipo.ro/ghid/cum-sa-trimiti-colet-la-locker)

### Inferences
- Budget about 13-20 lei + VAT (about 2.5-4 EUR) per domestic parcel for a small shop, plus the cost of COD refusals (return shipping, plus goods sometimes damaged).
- With order counts flat and growth driven by price, a new entrant has to take share from incumbents rather than ride market growth.
- Sameday/eMAG now control the largest courier and locker network as well as the largest marketplace. That is vertical concentration a small seller depends on but cannot negotiate with.
- Pushing card payment (for example a discount for paying online) is the main lever on returns: 3-5% vs 11-12%.

### Gaps
- I could not access the full GPeC 2025/2026 report or the ARMO annual study. There is no official national return rate.
- I found no published 2026 list prices from FAN Courier, DPD, or Cargus for small contracts, and no COD collection fee (% or fixed per parcel).
- Lockers per capita and total lockers across all networks in Romania (FANbox, Cargus Ship&Go, DPD, Packeta) were not found.

## 2. eMAG Marketplace (commissions, FBE, Genius, BG/HU), Allegro, Amazon EU access

### Takeaway
eMAG is the dominant channel and has no monthly fee by default (one source disagrees). The category commission is about 7-25%, and Fulfillment by eMAG (FBE) is cheap per unit. One account reaches Romania, Bulgaria, and Hungary. Selling on eMAG means competing on price and taking part in campaigns: more than 35% of marketplace sales come from campaigns. Allegro is **not** in Romania as of 2026. Amazon has no Romanian marketplace, but Romanian SRLs can sell on Amazon.de and the other EU stores.

### Cited Findings
**eMAG fees**
- Commission depends on category and ranges 7%-25% (eMAG seller page, also repeated by third-party guides). Another guide gives 5%-23% for 2026. An older "Open/Deschide România" model gave 5% for hardlines and 10% for softlines, which may be outdated — [eMAG Marketplace start-selling](https://marketplace.emag.ro/infocenter/start-selling/?lang=en); [Marqetir](https://marqetir.com/fees/emag-fees); [Fosetico KB](https://crm.fosetico.com/knowledge-base/article/emag)
- The same fee structure applies across RO/HU/BG through one seller panel, but legal documents are signed per country — [Zunapro](https://www.zunapro.com/hungary/en/blog/selling-emag-marketplace-guide); [Eurosell](https://eurosell.pl/strona/sprzedaz-na-emag)
- Monthly fee: Startarium says there is no fixed monthly fee. Shoply mentions a monthly subscription of 99-399 lei. The sources conflict — [Startarium](https://startarium.com/article/comisioane-rating-vizibilitate-emag-marketplace-ghid-rapid); [Shoply](https://shoply.ro/emag-marketplace-2026-analiza-selleri/)
- FBE: processing fee from 3.40 lei per unit, and the first 30 days of storage are free for each replenishment. Simulator examples: 1.9 lei for a smartwatch and 2.9 lei for a blender, with storage around 0.01-0.02 lei per unit — [eMAG FBE page](https://marketplace.emag.ro/emag-fbe/en). A competing 3PL estimates 5-7 lei all-in for a small product (commercial source) — [Fast FulFill](https://fastfulfill.ro/cat-costa-fulfillment-ul-in-romania-in-2026-ghid-complet-cu-formule-si-exemple/)
- FBE offers reach customers about twice as fast (about 1.5 days on average), and FBE grew 54%. Local sellers were offered 3 months of free FBE (promotion, details truncated) — [RevistaBiz](https://revistabiz.ro/fulfillment-by-emag-fbe-investitie-de-125-milioane-euro)
- Since 17 Apr 2026, eMAG charges **customers** an "operational services cost" of 1% of product value, minimum 1.49 lei and maximum 3.99 lei, on every order. eMAG says no action is required from sellers — [StartupCafe](https://startupcafe.ro/emag-noua-taxa-fiecare-comanda-platforma-explicatie-oficiala-98005); [Profit.ro](https://www.profit.ro/povesti-cu-profit/retail/emag-explica-taxa-pe-fiecare-comanda-ne-puseseram-in-buget-motorina-la-8-lei-22451072)

**eMAG scale, Genius, regional expansion**
- More than 64,000 marketplace sellers in 2024 (company-profile site, secondary) — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/flip-7)
- In 2025, sellers listed 31 million new products using AI tools, and 8,084 sellers started selling cross-border — [Economedia](https://economedia.ro/emag-zonele-afectate-de-concedieri-compania-anunta-un-buget-de-12-miliarde-de-lei-pentru-2026-si-investitii-in-tehnologie.html)
- 2024 baseline: more than 10,000 Romanian sellers sold cross-border into BG/HU for 552 M lei in cross-border sales (+40%), with 15 M offers in Bulgaria and 17 M in Hungary — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/flip-7)
- Sellers' Day 2026 (24 Sep 2026): sellers expanding regionally rose 25% YoY, and sellers using all 5 eMAG development programs had 41% higher sales than those using none — [Bursa](https://www.bursa.ro/sellers-day-2026-numarul-sellerilor-care-se-extind-regional-prin-emag-marketplace-a-crescut-cu-25-procente-in-ultimul-an-28273066)
- More than 35% of marketplace sales come from campaigns, and campaign sales grew 45% YoY — [News București](https://newsbucuresti.ro/sellers-day-2026-numarul-sellerilor-care-se-extind-regional-prin-emag-marketplace-a-crescut-cu-25-in-ultimul-an/)
- Genius has more than 1.5 M users regionally, who order about 7x more than non-members. Top active categories for women-led sellers were Home & Garden, consumables, and fashion — [Retail-FMCG](https://www.retail-fmcg.ro/e-commerce-2/afaceri-antreprenoare-emag-marketplace-crestere.html); [ZF](https://www.zf.ro/business-hi-tech/antreprenoarele-emag-marketplace-au-generat-vanzari-22-mari-ultimul-23082513)
- Genius restructured from 1 Oct 2026 into Light and Premium tiers. The current annual plan becomes Premium — [StartupCafe](https://startupcafe.ro/anunt-emag-abonamentul-genius-se-schimba-va-fi-inlocuit-de-3-versiuni-preturi-beneficii-si-ce-se-intampla-cu-genius-family-106864)
- Sellers pay a "Genius contribution" on Genius orders. eMAG has a dedicated page; the amount was not retrieved — [eMAG Marketplace infocenter](https://marketplace.emag.ro/infocenter/emag-academy/emag-genius/how-the-genius-contribution-is-applied/?lang=en)
- eMAG group budget: 1.2 bn lei of investment for the fiscal year, focused on AI, logistics, and financial services — [Playtech](https://playtech.ro/2026/emag-pariaza-12-miliarde-de-lei-pe-ai-logistica-si-servicii-financiare-ce-urmeaza-pentru-comertul-online-din-romania/)

**Allegro**
- ZF (May 2026): "Allegro creates a single East-European market from which Romania is missing". Allegro is entering Ukraine (deliveries from Poland from June 2026, allegro.ua in 2027) — [ZF](https://www.zf.ro/business-international/cea-mai-mare-platforma-de-comert-electronic-din-polonia-vrea-sa-23160756)
- Allegro operates in PL, CZ, SK, HU, HR, SI — [Ukrainska Pravda/Mezha](https://mezha.ua/en/news/allegro-zahodit-v-ukrajinu-311646/amp/)
- Allegro category commission is 1-17% (third-party). In 2025 it dropped listing and per-transaction fees but raised some category rates. From 2 Mar 2026 the Smart! delivery surcharge sellers pay scales with order value. Ads cost about 0.40-0.60 zł per click — [Shopappy](https://shopappy.com/trade/regional-marketplaces/allegro-smart-who-pays-seller-fees); [Sello](https://www.sello.io/post/how-to-sell-on-allegro-2026)

**Amazon**
- There is no Amazon marketplace for Romania: amazon.ro redirects to amazon.de. In 2025 more than 100,000 EU SMEs sold over 40 bn EUR on Amazon, 17 bn EUR of it cross-border. The number of Romanian SMEs selling grew in 2025 (no figure retrieved) — [Wall-Street.ro](https://www.wall-street.ro/articol/ecommerce/exclusiv-ce-spune-amazon-despre-comertul-online-din-romania-cati-romani-vand.html)
- Unofficial guide: Amazon EU referral fees are about 8-15%, and an SRL is recommended over a PFA — [InfoContact](https://www.infocontact.ro/cum-sa-vinzi-pe-amazon-din-romania/)
- An Antena 3 sponsored profile describes a Romanian Amazon brand-resale seller near Frankfurt who also runs a prep center (paid content, unverified claims) — [Antena 3](https://www.antena3.ro/continut-platit/andrei-faur-viziunea-unui-roman-care-schimba-regulile-jocului-pe-amazon-743750.html)

### Inferences
- Rough eMAG unit economics: on a 150-lei item, a 15% commission is about 22.5 lei and FBE processing about 2-3.5 lei, plus any Genius contribution and eMAG Ads. A typical 30-40% gross margin on resale goods can shrink to 10-15% before returns, ads, and taxes. Whether the commission is computed on the VAT-inclusive price is unverified.
- eMAG is the lowest-capital way to get traffic in Romania, but campaign dependence (>35% of sales) and price matching squeeze margins. The 1% customer fee may slightly lower conversion on cheap items.
- eMAG HU/BG from the same panel is the cheapest cross-border expansion route for a Romanian solo seller. Allegro is not an option for selling to Romanians. It would only be an export route into PL/CZ/HU and would need local-language listings and logistics.
- Amazon.de is accessible but competitive (EU sellers plus Chinese sellers). It suits branded/private-label or retail-arbitrage sellers with capital for inventory and FBA, and goes beyond a 2-3k EUR start.

### Gaps
- I could not retrieve the official eMAG commission table by category for 2026, the Genius contribution amount, or eMAG Ads CPC levels (marketplace.emag.ro was blocked).
- Total eMAG seller count for 2025/2026, and the share of sellers who are profitable, were not found.
- I found no reliable Romanian solo-seller case studies with audited eMAG margins.

## 3. Dropshipping, ad costs, consumer protection, EU/RO customs changes and Temu/Shein pressure

### Takeaway
China-sourced dropshipping into Romania is structurally weaker in 2026. Each sub-150-EUR non-EU parcel now pays a Romanian 25-lei fee (from 1 Jan 2026) plus the EU's 3 EUR duty per item category (from 1 Jul 2026). An EU handling fee of about 2 EUR is expected from about Nov 2026, possibly replacing the Romanian fee. These same charges narrow Temu/Shein's price advantage, but Temu (4.7 M Romanian monthly users) also stocks locally. Local/EU-supplier dropshipping avoids the fees but faces 14-day free returns, COD refusals, and Meta CPMs of about 15-45 lei.

### Cited Findings
**Customs and parcel fees**
- Romania charges a fixed 25 lei logistics fee per non-EU parcel under 150 EUR, collected by couriers (Law 239/2025), in force from 1 Jan 2026 after a CCR delay from 1 Nov 2025 — [Mobilissimo](https://www.mobilissimo.ro/stiri-diverse/romania-introduce-o-taxa-de-25-lei-pentru-coletele-ieftine-din-afara-ue-intra-in-vigoare-de-la-1-ianuarie-2026); [Profit.ro](https://profit.ro/taxe-si-consultanta/romania-impune-taxa-pe-colete-shein-temu-alibaba-22268733)
- ANAF collected about 167 M lei from the fee on 6,672,392 parcels in Jan-Mar 2026 — [Wall-Street.ro](https://www.wall-street.ro/articol/ecommerce/cati-bani-a-strans-anaf-din-taxa-temu-si-shein-de-la-curieri-in-primele-luni.html)
- Shein passes about 25.46 lei on to customers, and the fee is not refunded on returns — [Wall-Street.ro](https://www.wall-street.ro/articol/ecommerce/romanii-resimt-deja-taxa-de-25-de-lei-pe-colete-shein-a-inclus-o-in-pret.html). Temu adds 25 RON per order of 145 EUR or less shipped from outside the EU — [Wall-Street.ro](https://www.wall-street.ro/articol/ecommerce/temu-a-introdus-taxa-romaneasca-de-25-de-lei-pentru-produsele-din-afara-ue.html)
- EU: the 150 EUR duty exemption was removed, and a temporary flat 3 EUR duty per tariff line (6-digit HS code) applies to parcels under 150 EUR from 1 Jul 2026 to 1 Jul 2028, possibly extended. Example: a T-shirt plus shoes pays 2 × 3 EUR — [MarketScreener](https://uk.marketscreener.com/news/eu-to-impose-3-euro-duty-on-small-e-commerce-parcels-from-july-2026-ce7d50d8d98df723); [Adevărul](https://adevarul.ro/economie/cat-vei-plati-in-plus-pentru-comenzile-de-pe-temu-2534292.html)
- EY Romania: the 25-lei fee and the 3 EUR duty can stack because they are legally different — [News18](https://www.news18.ro/news/cum-se-calculeaza-pretul-coletelor-temu-si-shein-de-la-1-iulie-2026-apare-o-noua-taxa-ue-pe-langa-cei-25-de-lei-din-romania-50571.html)
- A European Commission spokesperson said Romania can keep its own fee only until an EU parcel-handling fee takes effect, "at the latest in November" 2026. The Finance Ministry's decision was left to the next government — [Profit.ro](https://www.profit.ro/taxe-si-consultanta/exclusiv-taxa-colete-afara-ue-romania-aplica-propria-taxa-25-lei-cel-tarziu-pana-noiembrie-1-iulie-apare-taxa-3-euro-nivel-ue-iar-cel-tarziu-noiembrie-vine-doua-taxa-europeana-22485391); [Profit.ro](https://www.profit.ro/taxe-si-consultanta/exclusiv-taxa-pe-colete-din-afara-ue-finantele-analizeaza-scenarii-dar-o-decizie-va-fi-luata-doar-de-urmatorul-guvern-de-la-1-iulie-la-taxa-romaneasca-de-25-de-lei-se-adauga-si-o-taxa-ue-22498595)
- The EU handling fee is reported at about 2 EUR per parcel from 1 Nov 2026. Some sources call it adopted; others say amount and date are still being negotiated — [FH Tax Law](https://fhtaxlaw.ro/taxe-colete-temu-shein-aliexpress-2026); [RSM](https://www.rsm.global/belgium/en/insights/eu-introduces-new-charges-low-value-imports)

**Temu/Shein competitive pressure**
- Temu reported 4.7 M monthly Romanian users at 30 Jun 2025, +20.5% vs the prior six months, the largest growth in the EU — [ECOMpedia](https://ecompedia.ro/temu-47-milioane-utilizatori-romani-s1-2025/); [CursDeGuvernare (ARMO's Cristian Pelivan)](https://cursdeguvernare.ro/cristian-pelivan-armo-conferinta-cdg-temu-utilizatori-romania-comert-online.html)
- ARMO survey of 511 companies: 77% report a negative impact from non-EU platforms — [Știri de Cluj](https://www.stiridecluj.ro/economic/romanii-cumpara-mai-scump-nu-mai-des-adevarul-din-spatele-cresterii-de-6-a-comertului-electronic-din-romania-si-ce-platforme-domina-piata-in-2026)
- Heureka/Compari study: about 73% of Romanian users of these platforms buy only once every few months or less, and low price is the main motive (84%) — [Retail.ro](https://retail.ro/articole/stiri-si-noutati/romanii-sunt-atrasi-de-preturile-mici-pe-temu-shein-si-aliexpress-dar-cumpara-mai-rar-decat-vecinii-15211.html). Only 3 in 10 Romanian buyers report no problems with Shein/Temu/AliExpress purchases — [StartupCafe](https://startupcafe.ro/3-10-cumparatori-romani-nu-probleme-achizitii-platforme-shein-temu-aliexpress-studiu-105582)
- Temu opened a local warehouse in Romania and offers faster delivery on some products (early 2025) — [nwradu blog](https://www.nwradu.ro/2025/02/temu-deschis-depozit-local-romania-devine-amenintare-mare/); [MoneyBuzz](https://moneybuzz.ro/livrari-mai-rapide-in-romania-pentru-anumite-produse-comandate-de-pe-temu/)

**Ad costs**
- Romanian benchmark guide: Meta (FB/IG) CPM 15-45 lei and CPC 0.5-3 lei, up 40-80% in peak season (Black Friday, holidays). E-commerce fashion is among the cheaper verticals — [Dafe.ro](https://dafe.ro/en/blog/cpm-cpc-benchmark-romania)
- Alternative estimate for Romania in 2026: Facebook CPM 2.50-4.00 USD and Instagram 3.50-5.50 USD (this conflicts with the lei range above) — [Adligator](https://adligator.com/blog/meta-ads-cpm-by-country-benchmarks)
- Global e-commerce CPA benchmarks (not Romania-specific): Meta median about 38 USD per purchase (Triple Whale 2025). TikTok median CPA 13.46-31.25 USD by vertical. TikTok minimum budgets are about 50 USD/day per campaign — [Influee](https://influee.co/ro/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks); [Influee TikTok](https://influee.co/ro/blog/tiktok-ads-benchmarks)
- Gomag: Romanian shop conversion is about 1-2%. Rule of thumb: spend 15-25% of revenue on marketing, more at launch — [Gomag blog](https://www.gomag.ro/blog/cati-bani-castigi-cu-magazin-online/)

**Consumer protection and legal**
- 14-day no-reason withdrawal for online purchases (OUG 34/2014). Exceptions: perishables, hygiene-sealed products that were unsealed, and **personalized** goods — [Avocatnet (ANPC guide)](https://www.avocatnet.ro/t592/anpc.html)
- Dropshipping requires a registered business (CUI). Non-EU suppliers bring customs and VAT obligations, and the seller stays liable to the customer for non-delivery — [Playtech](https://playtech.ro/2025/de-la-0-la-10-000-de-lei-lunar-cu-dropshipping-cum-sa-vinzi-legal-produse-fara-sa-le-ai-in-stoc-fara-sa-pui-mana-pe-ele/); [Ecommerce-platforms](https://ecommerce-platforms.com/ro/articles/best-print-on-demand-companies)

### Inferences
- Implied Romanian Meta CPA: CPC 0.5-3 lei divided by 1-2% conversion gives roughly 25-300 lei per purchase, so about 100 lei is a plausible mid case. On a 150-200 lei product with 40% gross margin, paid-social dropshipping is barely viable. It needs AOV of about 250+ lei, bundles, or organic/TikTok traffic.
- A 20-EUR Temu-style China parcel with two item types now carries about 4.8 EUR (25 lei) plus 6 EUR in duty, plus 21% import VAT. That makes "China dropship to Romanian customers" (2-3 week shipping plus fees) close to uncompetitive. It also helps local sellers of cheap goods compete against Temu/Shein cross-border orders, though not against Temu's local warehouse stock.
- "From 0 to 10,000 lei/month with dropshipping" type articles are hype. No independent data on Romanian dropshippers' success rates was found.
- Local-supplier dropshipping (Romanian distributors shipping white-label) avoids the parcel fees but gives thin margins (supplier price plus courier), and COD refusals land on the merchant.

### Gaps
- I found no named list of Romanian dropshipping supplier platforms with verified terms (the search returned only generic guides).
- Romania-specific CPA data for Meta/TikTok, and TikTok Shop availability in Romania in 2026, were not found.
- The final legal status of the EU 2 EUR handling fee, and whether Romania kept its 25-lei fee after Nov 2026, are unconfirmed.

## 4. Subscription and replenishment e-commerce (coffee, pet food, supplements)

### Takeaway
I found little evidence of Romanian D2C subscription businesses at scale. The visible examples are specialty-coffee roasters (2020-era coverage) and big players' auto-delivery (Zooplus). eMAG Genius (free delivery, more than 1.5 M members) and pet chains compete on convenience. Replenishment is realistic mainly as a niche own-brand add-on, not as a standalone 2-3k EUR/month model, unless the operator already has an audience.

### Cited Findings
- xpresscups (Bucharest, 2020): monthly specialty-coffee subscription with 4 varieties (beans or ground) and a community. It raised a 10,000 EUR investment. Current status unknown — [StartupCafe](https://startupcafe.ro/startup-romanesc-livreaza-cafea-investitie-htm-13488); [RevistaBiz](https://www.revistabiz.ro/un-startup-romanesc-livreaza-cafea-de-specialitate-pe-baza-de-abonament/)
- Olivo Coffee Roasters (Cluj) said in 2020 that coffee subscriptions were among its most requested services. It launched Romanian-made specialty capsules from 26.60 lei for 14 capsules — [Economica.net](https://www.economica.net/au-fost-lansate-primele-capsule-cu-cafea-de-specialitate-produse-in-romania_193769.html/amp)
- La Fântâna offers B2B office coffee subscriptions (machine plus periodic capsules plus maintenance) — [Avocatnet](https://www.avocatnet.ro/articol_39132/P-La-Fantana-ofera-o-gama-completa-de-abonamente-de-cafea-pentru-birou.html)
- Zooplus Romania offers subscription/auto-delivery for pet food, litter, and medication — [search extract](https://grandavehousing.calpoly.edu/news/zooplus-romania-app-your-pet) (low-quality aggregator; treat as indicative)
- Pet market context: about every second Romanian household owns a cat (FEDIAF). Fressnapf (in Romania since 2022) plans more than 100 stores — [Kormotech](https://kormotech.com/articles/kormotech-posilue-prisutnist-u-rumunii-produkcia-z-avilasa-v-merezi-fressnapf)
- Consumables are one of the most active eMAG Marketplace categories (along with Home & Garden and fashion) — [ZF](https://www.zf.ro/business-hi-tech/antreprenoarele-emag-marketplace-au-generat-vanzari-22-mari-ultimul-23082513)
- Genius members order about 7x more than non-members, with more than 1.5 M members regionally — [Retail-FMCG](https://www.retail-fmcg.ro/e-commerce-2/afaceri-antreprenoare-emag-marketplace-crestere.html)

### Inferences
- Commodity replenishment (pet food, mainstream supplements) runs straight into eMAG Genius free shipping, Zooplus, and pet chains on price and delivery. A solo operator can't win on price or logistics.
- Viable angle: an own-brand or curated niche such as a specialty-coffee roaster partnership, a niche diet pet food, or a local artisanal product. Recurring card billing helps there, because it avoids COD and lowers returns. Courier costs of about 13-17 lei per box mean the box value should be at least about 100-150 lei to keep shipping under 10-15% of revenue.
- Food and supplements bring regulatory overhead (ANSVSA/food-safety notification, supplement notification). That was not researched here; see Gaps.

### Gaps
- I found no current (2025-2026) data on Romanian subscription e-commerce adoption, churn, or named profitable D2C subscription brands.
- Regulatory requirements for selling supplements or pet food online in Romania were not researched.
- Recurring card-payment support among Romanian processors (NETOPIA, EuPlatesc, Stripe) was not verified.

## 5. Print-on-demand (POD) from Romanian/EU providers

### Takeaway
At least one Romanian POD/dropshipping printer exists: Printoteca, in Bucharest since 2015 (DTG, automated via The Brick Factory). Global networks such as Gelato route orders to local printers. POD avoids inventory, but margins depend on design and audience. Personalized items may fall under the 14-day withdrawal exception, which reduces return risk.

### Cited Findings
- Printoteca (Bucharest, founded 2015): DTG printing, POD and dropshipping services automated via The Brick Factory, with Stanley/Stella premium textiles (company's own site) — [Printoteca](https://portal.printoteca.ro/about-us); [Brickweb](https://www.brickweb.co.uk/print-on-demand-the-brick-factory-in-europe)
- Gelato routes orders to local print partners. A Romanian facility was not confirmed. Printful and Printify are covered in comparisons, but no Romanian print partner was confirmed — [Ecommerce-platforms](https://ecommerce-platforms.com/ro/articles/best-print-on-demand-companies)
- SmileFoto.ro offers custom T-shirt design and upload (paid/sponsored article) — [Antena 3](https://www.antena3.ro/continut-platit/tricourile-personalizate-au-cucerit-romania-de-ce-nu-mai-sunt-doar-o-moda-ci-un-statement-740574.html)
- Personalized goods are excluded from the 14-day withdrawal right — [Avocatnet (ANPC guide)](https://www.avocatnet.ro/t592/anpc.html)

### Inferences
- POD for Romanian buyers is realistic as a low-capital test (no stock). The economics are about blank plus print cost, plus about 15 lei courier, plus ads. Without an existing audience (community, niche humor, local pride, sports clubs, company merch), paid acquisition at about 100 lei CPA rarely fits a 80-120 lei T-shirt.
- Personalized products (names, photos) carry less return risk and are a better fit than generic designs. AI image tools can speed up design work, but IP/trademark risk (football clubs, brands) is a real failure mode.

### Gaps
- Printoteca base prices, other Romanian POD printers, and their API integrations with Shopify/WooCommerce/Gomag were not retrieved.
- I found no data on Romanian POD seller earnings.

## 7. What can be automated with AI vs what needs capital and manual work

### Takeaway
AI clearly cuts the cost of catalog and creative work. eMAG reports 31 M products listed with AI tools in 2025. It does not remove the capital- and labor-heavy parts of physical e-commerce: inventory, COD refusals, returns, courier problems, and price competition.

### Cited Findings
- eMAG sellers listed 31 million new products using AI-based tools in 2025 — [Economedia](https://economedia.ro/emag-zonele-afectate-de-concedieri-compania-anunta-un-buget-de-12-miliarde-de-lei-pentru-2026-si-investitii-in-tehnologie.html)
- eMAG is investing 1.2 bn lei focused on AI, logistics, and financial services — [Playtech](https://playtech.ro/2026/emag-pariaza-12-miliarde-de-lei-pe-ai-logistica-si-servicii-financiare-ce-urmeaza-pentru-comertul-online-din-romania/)
- UGC/creative cost drives ad performance. Comparing cost per result, not CPM, is recommended — [Influee](https://influee.co/ro/blog/tiktok-cpm)
- The dropship/POD seller remains responsible for customer support and non-delivery complaints — [Ecommerce-platforms](https://ecommerce-platforms.com/ro/articles/best-print-on-demand-companies)
- One refused COD parcel offsets about 3 good orders. Refusal handling is an operational cost — [Ziare.com](https://ziare.com/e-commerce/gaura-neagra-a-e-commerce-ului-peste-12-la-suta-din-profitul-magazinelor-online-dispare-pe-coletele-refuzate-2003830)

### Inferences
- Automatable with AI (low marginal cost): product titles and descriptions in RO/HU/BG for eMAG cross-border, image cleanup and lifestyle images, ad creative variants and UGC-style videos, first-line customer support (order status, FAQs), review analysis, and pricing monitoring.
- Not automatable (capital and labor): buying and holding stock (or FBE replenishment), packing (unless FBE/3PL), COD refusal and return processing, warranty/ANPC complaints, courier disputes, cash-flow gaps (marketplace payout terms vs supplier payment), and accounting/e-Factura compliance.
- Because AI tools are available to every seller, catalog creation is no longer a moat. Competition shifts to price, logistics speed (FBE, easybox), and brand.

### Gaps
- No independent Romanian data on AI's measured effect on small-seller margins or conversion.
- eMAG's specific AI seller tools (names, costs) were not retrieved.

## 8. Capital requirements, failure modes, realistic net margins after ads, returns, courier and tax

### Takeaway
Reaching 2,000-3,000 EUR/month **net** from physical e-commerce in Romania typically takes about 15-25k EUR in monthly revenue (about 200-650 orders/month depending on per-order profit). At that size the business is above the VAT threshold and above the 100k EUR micro-company ceiling, and it needs far more working capital than 2-3k EUR, except for no-stock models (POD, local dropship), which have thinner margins. It is achievable but slow and capital-hungry compared with digital or service models.

### Cited Findings
- Tax framework 2026: VAT standard rate 21% from 1 Aug 2025 (Law 141/2025), reduced rate 11%. The VAT registration threshold is 395,000 lei of annual turnover (OG 22/2025) — [Bono](https://bono.ro/blog/modificari-fiscale-cum-se-schimba-tva-cass-impozitul-pe-dividende); [Fiscalitatea](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/plafoane-fiscale-2026-24595/)
- The microenterprise regime in 2026 has a single 1% rate (OUG 89/2025) and a 100,000 EUR turnover ceiling, and requires at least 1 full-time employee or a mandate-paid administrator. Fiscalitatea.ro still mentions a 3% rate above 60k EUR, which looks outdated — [Contzilla](https://www.contzilla.ro/microintreprinderea-in-2026/); [Bono](https://bono.ro/blog/impozit-micro-obligatii-fiscale-pentru-microintreprinderi); [Fiscalitatea](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/plafoane-fiscale-2026-24595/)
- Exceeding 100k EUR triggers new tax obligations (exit from the micro regime) — [Contabilul.manager.ro](https://contabilul.manager.ro/a/30084/microintreprindere-ce-obligatii-fiscale-apar-dupa-depasirea-plafonului-de-100000-euros.html)
- Marketing typically takes 15-25% of revenue, and conversion is 1-2% — [Gomag blog](https://www.gomag.ro/blog/cati-bani-castigi-cu-magazin-online/)
- Shipo example: a 300-order/month store losing 750 lei to refusals equals 12.5% of net profit, which implies about 6,000 lei (about 1,150 EUR) net/month, or about 20 lei net per order — [Ziare.com](https://ziare.com/e-commerce/gaura-neagra-a-e-commerce-ului-peste-12-la-suta-din-profitul-magazinelor-online-dispare-pe-coletele-refuzate-2003830)
- AOV is about 452 lei (Gomag 2025). COD is 60-65%. COD return rate is 11-12% vs 3-5% for card — [ReturnLab](https://returnlab.io/blog/retururi-romania-2026-ghid-complet)
- Market transactions are flat and growth is price-driven — [Știri de Cluj (MerchantPro)](https://www.stiridecluj.ro/economic/romanii-cumpara-mai-scump-nu-mai-des-adevarul-din-spatele-cresterii-de-6-a-comertului-electronic-din-romania-si-ce-platforme-domina-piata-in-2026). 77% of ARMO-surveyed firms report negative impact from non-EU platforms — [same](https://www.stiridecluj.ro/economic/romanii-cumpara-mai-scump-nu-mai-des-adevarul-din-spatele-cresterii-de-6-a-comertului-electronic-din-romania-si-ce-platforme-domina-piata-in-2026)

### Inferences
**Worked example: own store, VAT payer, inputs taken from the cited figures above**
- AOV 450 lei gross is about 372 lei net of 21% VAT. Assumed COGS of 50% of net price is 186 lei. Courier is about 15 lei. Ads at 20% of net revenue are about 74 lei. Refusals and returns at about 4% are about 15 lei. Contribution is therefore about 80 lei (about 15 EUR) per order before fixed costs (platform and apps about 100 EUR, accounting, tools) and tax.
- To net 2,500 EUR (about 13,100 lei) after profit tax, you need about 200-230 orders/month at 80 lei, or about 650 at the 20 lei/order implied by the Shipo example. That is roughly 18-55k EUR monthly revenue, or 7-22 orders/day.
- Consequence: this revenue (more than 200k EUR/year) exceeds both the 395k lei VAT threshold (about 75k EUR) and the 100k EUR micro ceiling. Plan for VAT and profit-tax-regime accounting. The 16% corporate profit tax is the standard rate, not re-verified this session.
- Working capital: holding even 2-4 weeks of stock for about 200 orders at 186 lei COGS is about 18-37k lei (3.5-7k EUR), plus ad spend paid upfront and COD money arriving with a delay. A 2-3k EUR start forces either no-stock models (POD, local dropship, lower margins) or a slow, profit-funded ramp.

**Model ranking for a solo operator with less than 3k EUR (inference)**
- (a) eMAG Marketplace reselling a narrow niche with FBE: traffic is provided, but margin is squeezed by 7-25% commission plus price wars. This is the best "first sales" channel.
- (b) Own niche store (Gomag/Shopify) with organic/TikTok content plus eMAG as a second channel.
- (c) POD/personalized gifts: low capital, seasonal, and needs audience/creative skill.
- (d) Replenishment/subscription: only with a differentiated own product.
- (e) China dropshipping to Romania: structurally disadvantaged after the 2026 fees and should be avoided.
- (f) Amazon.de/Allegro export: needs more capital and logistics; not a starter model.

**Typical failure modes (inference from the cited data)**
- Ad CPA higher than gross margin per order.
- COD refusals at 10-25% in impulse niches.
- Price wars on eMAG and with Temu's local stock.
- Inventory stuck after a failed product test.
- Cash-flow strain from VAT/tax and stock purchases.
- Owner burnout from manual returns and support.
- Platform dependency: eMAG rules and fees (e.g., the new April 2026 customer fee) and Meta ad-account bans.

**Comparison note for digital/service models**
- Physical e-commerce here earns about 5-15% net margin on revenue, versus typically much higher for digital or service models. The target requires 7-25x more gross revenue than net income.
