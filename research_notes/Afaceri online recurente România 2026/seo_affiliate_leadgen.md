# Niche sites, SEO/affiliate, directories, programmatic SEO and local lead-gen (rank-and-rent) for a solo operator in Romania, target 2,000-3,000 EUR/month (state as of Oct 2026)

> Method note for the report writer: the research environment blocked direct page fetches (egress proxy refused most hosts), so the facts below come from search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not full-page reads. Figures marked "(secondary)" were relayed by an aggregator rather than the original publisher. Treat exact decimals with appropriate caution. Approximate conversion used in inferences: 1 EUR ≈ 5 RON (rounded, not a sourced rate).

## 1. Impact of AI Overviews / AI Mode / LLM search and Google policy changes (2025-2026), incl. Romanian rollout

### Takeaway
Every major dataset shows a large cut in organic clicks wherever an AI Overview (AIO) appears: roughly -35% to -60% CTR at position 1, and AI Mode sessions are ~93% zero-click. A partial CTR rebound was seen in early 2026, but clicks stayed well below the no-AIO baseline. Romania has had AIO since about May 2025 and AI Mode in Romanian since 8 October 2025. Romanian search traffic was down ~18% YoY in Aug 2026. Informational content sites are the most exposed; transactional and local-pack queries much less so.

### Cited Findings
**Global CTR studies**
- Ahrefs (update published 4 Feb 2026, Dec 2025 data): AIOs cut position-1 organic CTR by 58%. Average pos-1 CTR for AIO keywords fell from 0.073 (Dec 2023) to 0.016 (Dec 2025). The effect shrinks lower down: pos-2 -50.8%, pos-10 -19.4%. Ahrefs' April 2025 version of the study had found -34.5% — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/)
- Seer Interactive, 3rd AIO CTR study (published April 2026; 53 brands, 5.47M queries, 2.43B impressions; actuals Jan 2025–Feb 2026). AIO organic CTRs "rebound 85% in the first two months of 2026" after a year of near-continuous compression. Seer calls the data directional and says the old search environment is not back — [Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/blog/aio-impact-on-google-ctr-2026-update)
  - Underlying numbers (secondary): AIO-query organic CTR ~1.3% (Dec 2025) → ~2.4% (Feb 2026), against ~3.8% for non-AIO queries in Feb 2026. Brands cited in the AIO get ~120% more clicks per impression than uncited ones but still trail no-AIO queries by ~38% — [ALM Corp](https://almcorp.com/blog/google-ai-overviews-organic-ctr-2026/); [presenc.ai](https://presenc.ai/research/ai-overviews-ctr-impact-2026)
  - Seer's 2025 study (secondary): informational-query organic CTR with AIO fell 1.76% → 0.61% (-61%); paid CTR fell 19.7% → 6.34% — [seomatic.ai summary](https://seomatic.ai/resources/ai-overviews-ctr-study)
- Pew Research Center (22 Jul 2025): 900 US adults, browsing data for March 2025. Users clicked a traditional result in 8% of visits when an AI summary appeared vs 15% without one. Sources cited inside the summary were clicked in only ~1% of visits. Sessions ended after 26% of AI-summary pages vs 16% of plain result pages (last figure per a Medium recap). Pew shows correlation, not causation — [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears/); [ODSC/Medium](https://odsc.medium.com/pew-study-google-users-click-less-when-ai-summaries-appear-in-search-results-cc003baf6a35)
- Semrush (Datos clickstream, ~69M US desktop sessions, 1 May–5 Jul 2025): ~92–94% of AI Mode sessions were zero-click; only 6–8% led to an external site. Semrush's earlier figure for classic Search/AIO was 35–46% zero-click — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/google-ai-mode-seo-impact/)
- SparkToro (Similarweb clickstream, Jan–Apr 2026): 68.01% of US Google searches ended with no click, up from 60.45% in 2024. Only 0.34% of searches transitioned into AI Mode — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/google-zero-click-searches-2026-study-479717); [TechWyse](https://www.techwyse.com/news/reports-data/google-zero-click-searches-68-percent-2026)
- Similarweb via Digiday: search referrals to 1,000 large domains fell from 12.0B (Jun 2024) to 11.2B (Jun 2025), about -6.7%. Zero-click on news searches rose 56% → 69% (May 2024 → May 2025). AI-platform referrals are growing but do not offset the loss — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/in-graphic-detail-ai-platforms-are-driving-more-traffic-but-not-enough-to-offset-zero-click-search/)
- Chartbeat (March 2026, secondary): over two years, search referrals fell -60% for small sites (<10k daily pageviews), -47% for mid-size and -22% for large outlets. Small publishers are hit hardest — [seo-day.de](https://www.seo-day.de/news/article/referral-traffic-is-declining-for-smaller-publishers-what?lang=en)

**Which query types get AIOs**
- Semrush 600k-keyword study: over 6 months, commercial-intent SERPs showing AIOs rose 71% while transactional-intent ones fell 5%. Finance had the largest commercial growth, +231% — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-overviews-commercial-search-study/)
- Semrush 10M-keyword study (secondary summary): informational queries' share of AIO triggers fell from 91.3% (Jan 2025) to 57.1% (Oct 2025). Commercial rose 8.15% → 18.57%, transactional 1.98% → 13.94%. This conflicts with the 600k study on transactional queries; the two likely measure different metrics — [Stanventures](https://www.stanventures.com/news/semrush-ai-overviews-study-google-search-seo-in-2025-6318.md.txt)
- AIOs appeared in ~43% of US searches in May 2026, up from ~15% in Jan 2025 (secondary, Spanish-language summary of Similarweb) — [ecosistemastartup](https://ecosistemastartup.com/?p=94573)

**LLM search (ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini)**
- Similarweb 2026 GenAI report (secondary):
  - Scale: AI chatbots average 655M monthly unique visitors vs ~3.3B for traditional search. 461M of ChatGPT's 494M users also used Google (Mar–May 2026).
  - Chatbot share: ChatGPT's share of AI-assistant visits fell to 53.9% (May 2026) from 79% a year earlier; Gemini 27.9%.
  - Referrals: US AI-platform referrals to external sites were 226.8M in Jan 2026, down 15% from 267.4M in Oct 2025.
  - Links in answers: the share of US ChatGPT answers with an external link rose from ~1.6% (Jun 2025) to 6.8% (May 2026).
  - [Search Engine Journal](https://searchenginejournal.com/ai-search-isnt-replacing-google-its-layering-on-top-similarweb-data/583378/); [ecosistemastartup](https://ecosistemastartup.com/?p=94573)

**Romania-specific**
- AI Mode ("Modul AI") launched in Romania, in Romanian, on 8 Oct 2025. It runs on Gemini 2.5, rolled out gradually on desktop and the Android/iOS apps, and is a separate tab, not a replacement for classic results — [Economedia](https://economedia.ro/google-anunta-disponibilitatea-in-romania-si-pentru-limba-romana-a-modului-ai.html); [Forbes România](https://www.forbes.ro/google-lanseaza-in-romania-modul-ai-cautare-bazata-pe-inteligenta-artificiala-in-limba-romana-468812); [StartupCafe](https://startupcafe.ro/google-modul-ai-functie-disponibila-in-google-search-ajunge-si-in-romania-cum-poate-ajuta-firmele-87723); [Profit.ro](https://www.profit.ro/povesti-cu-profit/it-c/google-lanseaza-in-romania-cautarile-in-modul-ai-foto-22192508)
- AI Overviews in Romania: one Romanian SEO guide dates the launch to 20 May 2025, the same day Google announced at I/O that AIO reached 200+ countries and 40+ languages. Google's post, as surfaced, does not explicitly list Romanian, and the Romanian date has a single (agency) source — [Startarium](https://startarium.com/article/ghid-complet-google-ai-overviews-pozitia-0-2025); [Google blog](https://blog.google/products/search/ai-overview-expansion-may-2025-update/); [re7consulting](https://re7consulting.ro/google-ai-overview-vine-si-in-romania-ce-schimba-pentru-utilizatori-si-afaceri/)
- DWF traffic dashboard (trafic.dwf.ro, reportedly 799 Romanian sites), August 2026:
  - Search traffic -18.3% YoY; total traffic -7.1%; the decline is slowing.
  - Romanian e-commerce: organic sessions -15.9% while revenue +6.9%, i.e., the lost traffic is mostly informational.
  - AI assistants bring ~0.7% of sessions but convert better.
  - [Nationalul](https://www.nationalul.ro/2026/09/11/traficul-online-din-romania-isi-incetineste-scaderea-iar-ai-accelereaza-ce-arata-datele-actualizate-pe-trafic-dwf-ro/); [aisucces.ro](https://www.aisucces.ro/ai-mode-google-romania/)

**Google policies / updates**
- Scaled content abuse policy: introduced March 2024, enforced from May 2024. It covers any method (automated, human or hybrid) of mass-producing low-value content — [bulkbase.ai summary](https://bulkbase.ai/seo/understanding-googles-scaled-content-abuse-policy); [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-updates-site-reputation-abuse-policy-removes-penalties-in-eea/587423/)
- Site reputation abuse ("parasite SEO") change, effective 30 Aug 2026, after talks with the European Commission under the DMA:
  - Inside the EEA (which includes Romania) there will be no ranking-affecting manual actions. Instead, Google may algorithmically separate the offending section so it ranks "on its own merits".
  - Outside the EEA, manual actions still hit the violating section.
  - The policy definition itself was not changed.
  - [Google Search Central blog](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/08/update-site-reputation-policy); [Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-site-reputation-abuse-eea-41968.html); [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-updates-site-reputation-abuse-policy-removes-penalties-in-eea/587423/)
- Core and spam updates:
  - December 2025 core update: reported as hitting affiliate sites hard. One source says 71% of affiliate sites reported drops; that is a vendor-blog figure.
  - March 2026 core update: finished rolling out 8 Apr 2026. Affiliate was reported as the most-hit category, with "near-zero correlation" between AI assistance and losses; losses were attributed to unedited AI drafts at volume (single vendor blog).
  - August 2026 spam update: case studies include scaled/programmatic content losses; one YMYL site lost rankings for 200K+ queries (single consultant write-up).
  - [Kevin Leary](https://www.kevinleary.net/blog/googles-december-2025-core-update/); [getasa2](https://getasa2.com/blog/why-googles-march-2026-update-hit-affiliate-sites-hardest/); [GSQI](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/august-2026-google-spam-update-case-studies/); [Newor Media](https://newormedia.com/blog/?p=1519)

### Inferences
- For a new Romanian-language informational content site (how-to, "ce este", reviews that just paraphrase specs), expect position-1 CTR about half of the 2023 level. AIOs and AI Mode are live in Romanian, and Romanian search referrals are already down ~18% YoY.
- The Seer early-2026 rebound means the decline is not monotonic. Even so, AIO queries still deliver roughly 35–40% fewer clicks than non-AIO queries.
- Transactional and simple local queries are least affected, which favours the "buy now / book now / find provider near me" sub-models.
- Under the new EEA rule, Romanian "parasite SEO" will no longer bring manual penalties for EEA users. That does not create a durable opportunity: algorithmic demotion still applies, and non-EEA users still see manual actions.

### Gaps
- No Google primary source found confirming the exact date AI Overviews started showing in Romanian; the 20 May 2025 date is from one agency article.
- No Romanian-language CTR study (with vs without AIO) found; the DWF dashboard is the only local dataset, and its methodology could not be inspected.
- Seer's exact monthly CTR decimals and the 120%/38% citation figures could not be verified on Seer's own page (fetch blocked).

## 2. Which traffic models remain viable (directories/databases, tools/calculators, pSEO with unique data, local lead-gen, comparison, YouTube affiliate, email-list affiliate)

### Takeaway
The evidence is thin but consistent. Models built on proprietary or structured data, transactional or local intent, or an owned audience (email, YouTube) are holding up. Templated informational pages and thin pSEO are being deindexed or demoted. No independent, audited case studies of 2026 winners were found; most "what works" claims come from vendors.

### Cited Findings
- **Local intent:**
  - Whitespark (Q2 2025): AIOs appeared on 68% of local searches overall, but only ~15% of simple local queries (e.g., "tacos San Francisco"), where the local pack appears >90% of the time.
  - On the same study, informational local queries got AIOs 92% of the time and hybrid queries 97%. The local pack still dominates pure transactional and navigational local queries.
  - [Whitespark](https://whitespark.ca/blog/case-study-the-prevalence-of-ai-overviews-in-local-search/)
- **Romanian e-commerce:** organic sessions -15.9% but revenue +6.9% (Aug 2026), so commercial and transactional intent is converting despite less traffic — [Nationalul / DWF](https://www.nationalul.ro/2026/09/11/traficul-online-din-romania-isi-incetineste-scaderea-iar-ai-accelereaza-ce-arata-datele-actualizate-pe-trafic-dwf-ro/)
- **Programmatic SEO:**
  - The consistent pattern across sources: pages with proprietary or refreshed data survive, while keyword-swap templates get deindexed.
  - Anecdotes (unverified): a travel site with 50,000 "hotels in [city]" pages lost 98% of them from the index in 3 months. A vendor claims 87% of pages on 2,600 sites that lost traffic in the Aug 2025 core update had no first-party data.
  - A claimed "25–30% unique text/data per page" threshold is a vendor heuristic, not Google's.
  - Zapier's integration pages are the usual success example.
  - [quickseo.ai](https://quickseo.ai/blog/programmatic-seo-stats-2026-is-pseo-still-viable-in-the-ai-search-era); [thestacc](https://thestacc.com/blog/programmatic-seo-local-data/); [GSQI](https://www.gsqi.com/marketing-blog/august-2026-google-spam-update-case-studies/)
- **Directories:** vendor claims that niche directories with structured data, real reviews and curated listings are "thriving" post-AIO, and that AI systems pull from directories. These are promotional, with no measured data — [Directorist](https://directorist.com/blog/online-directory-business-model/); [aDirectory](https://adirectory.io/how-directory-websites-work/)
- **Exit-market signals of format resilience:** Flippa's 2025 review says content sites fell 33.5% in performance while SaaS and YouTube rose. It attributes this partly to AIOs cutting traffic to generic Q&A content — [Flippa](https://flippa.com/blog/2025-online-business-ma-insights-from-flippa/)
- **Owned audience:** buyers reportedly pay premiums for owned audiences (email) and traffic diversification, and stress-test listings for "-20% organic over two years" — [Newor Media](https://newormedia.com/blog/?p=1519)
- **Tools/calculators:** calculator sites' traffic mixes in Semrush show large direct shares (e.g., one site 53.47% direct, 31.47% google.com), which suggests repeat use. No study isolating AIO impact on tool sites was found — [Semrush calculator.net overview](https://de.semrush.com/website/calculator.net/overview/)
- **Comparison sites:** Romania's leading comparator Compari.ro (Heureka group) is shifting from pure CPC towards a marketplace model, targeting 12,000 active merchants by 2028 — [ZF](https://www.zf.ro/business-hi-tech/sorin-adrian-dochian-country-manager-compari-platforma-comparare-23181804)

### Inferences
Ranking of sub-models by defensibility for a solo Romanian operator (inference from the above):
1. **Local lead-gen / provider directories for transactional local services.** Low AIO exposure on "service + city" queries, and the local pack dominates. Caveat: the local pack is won by Google Business Profiles, not your site, so you must rank in the organic results below the pack.
2. **Databases/pSEO built on unique Romanian public data** (e.g., prices, registries, public procurement, regulated professions lists), structured so each page carries data not available in an AIO answer. This needs genuine data, not template filler.
3. **Interactive tools/calculators** in Romanian (taxes, salaries, ANAF forms, notary fees). Plausibly defensible because they deliver a computation, not text, but no direct evidence was found.
4. **YouTube or email-led affiliate.** Resilient to Google but limited by Romanian RPMs and eMAG's short cookie windows (see §3–4).
5. **Informational blog + affiliate/display.** The weakest model.

Comparison/price sites compete against Compari.ro, Google Shopping and eMAG itself, so they are unrealistic for a solo operator except in narrow verticals.

### Gaps
- No independent, audited 2025–2026 revenue/traffic case studies for directories, tools or pSEO sites (Romanian or global) found.
- No data on AIO frequency for Romanian-language queries by intent.

## 3. Romanian affiliate ecosystem (2Performant, Profitshare, eMAG, Altex, commissions, cookies, payouts, comparison sites)

### Takeaway
Two networks dominate. 2Performant is listed and independent. Profitshare stayed in-house at eMAG after the planned sale to 2Performant collapsed in Aug 2025. eMAG pays 1–20% commission on ex-VAT value with very short attribution windows (2–15 days; only 2 days for phones/TVs/laptops), which hurts content affiliates. The whole 2Performant network paid affiliates ~35M RON (~7M EUR) in 2025, flat vs 2024, which signals a small, mature market.

### Cited Findings
- **Profitshare / 2Performant deal:**
  - 2Performant agreed to buy Profitshare Romania and Bulgaria from eMAG's group for ~4.3–4.5M lei (cash plus 2Performant shares, which would have made eMAG a shareholder) — [StartupCafe](https://startupcafe.ro/firma-romaneasca-marketing-digital-2performant-achizitie-platforma-profitshare-4-5-milioane-lei-80819); [Profit.ro](https://www.profit.ro/povesti-cu-profit/emag-vinde-profitshare-romania-si-bulgaria-proprietarul-devine-actionar-la-2performant-intelegere-dupa-acord-preliminar-22024149)
  - The understanding signed in May 2025 was invalidated in early August 2025. eMAG kept Profitshare in-house, called the affiliate ecosystem a strategic priority and launched partner initiatives — [Mobilissimo](https://www.mobilissimo.ro/stiri-diverse/emag-mizeaza-pe-dezvoltarea-interna-a-profitshare-si-lanseaza-initiative-dedicate-partenerilor-in-urma-neconcretizarii-tranzactiei-cu-2performant-platforma-ramane-una-in-house)
- **eMAG on Profitshare:**
  - Commission grid valid from 27 Oct 2025: 1.00%–20.00% depending on category.
  - Attribution ("perioada de comisionare") is 2–15 days depending on product; 2 days for phones, TVs and laptops.
  - Commission is calculated on product value excluding VAT and shipping, on the price actually paid after discounts.
  - [Profitshare eMAG program](https://profitshare.ro/affiliate-programs/retail/emag); [Profitshare blog](https://blog.profitshare.ro/schimbari-afiliere-emag/)
- **eMAG extras (undated on the blog):**
  - Commission increases on a selection of categories.
  - A Black Friday rule of 1% commission for sales made on the Friday.
  - The "eLITE" creator program: 300€ for creators with 50k–100k followers, 500€ above 100k, and triple commission in Fashion, Beauty and Petshop with a 7-day attribution window.
  - [Profitshare blog](https://blog.profitshare.ro/); [Profitshare blog – updates](https://blog.profitshare.ro/emag-anunta-actualizari-importante-pentru-programul-de-afiliere-perioada-extinsa-de-recurenta-comisioane-mai-mari-si-bonusuri-de-black-friday/)
- **Profitshare scale:** 150,000+ registered affiliates; ~4bn lei of sales generated since 2009. It claims its best affiliates earn "zeci de mii de lei lunar" (marketing claim, unaudited) — [Profitshare](https://profitshare.ro/); [Maior Army](https://www.maiorarmy.ro/cum-poti-castiga-bani-online-promovand-produse-pe-care-oricum-le-recomanzi/)
- **2Performant terms:**
  - Payout threshold 25 EUR / 100 RON, paid weekly (Thursdays) by bank transfer.
  - Cookie length varies by program and renews on each click; a third-party directory says 30 days is typical.
  - Advertisers are advised to join only once they have 200–300 orders/month. The advertiser "Performer" plan costs €69/month.
  - [2Performant support – payouts](https://support.2performant.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000094711-when-and-how-are-commissions-paid-); [2Performant T&C](https://2performant.com/terms-conditions/); [PostAffiliatePro](https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/affiliate-program-directory/2performant-affiliate-program/); [2Performant advertisers](https://2performant.com/advertiseri-aplica/); [2Performant pricing](https://support.2performant.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000094832-what-are-the-current-pricing-plans-)
- **2Performant financials:**
  - 2025 preliminary results: commissions paid to affiliates 35,039,284 RON (2025) vs 35,032,451 RON (2024), 0% change. Total turnover ~48.04M RON, also flat. Network-commission revenue +5% and subscription revenue +16%.
  - The average network commission was 1.18 EUR/sale across the platform and 1.8 EUR/sale for clients launched in 2025. This is 2Performant's fee, not the affiliate's commission.
  - Q1 2025 operating revenue fell 10% to 3M lei.
  - [2Performant 2025 preliminary report (PDF)](https://2performant.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Raport-Rezultate-financiare-preliminare-2025_RO.pdf); [Financial Market](https://financialmarket.ro/piata-de-capital/market-news/platforma-de-marketing-afiliat-2performant-a-raportat-o-scadere-a-veniturilor-in-primul-trimestru/)
  - Conflict: a 2024 blog cites ~13M EUR paid to affiliates (vs 8.3M EUR the year before). That does not match the ~7M EUR implied by the 35M RON in the official reports; the blog figure may cover a different scope or period — [nwradu blog](https://www.nwradu.ro/2024/09/mutari-in-afiliere-2performant-va-achizitiona-retelele-profitshare-romania-si-bulgaria/)
- **Altex / Flanco (old data):**
  - Altex: 3% commission and a one-month recurrence window on 2Parale (2011, now outdated) — [Gadget.ro](https://gadget.ro/altex-intra-in-sistemul-de-afiliere-2parale/)
  - Flanco relaunched its affiliate program on Profitshare in 2020 — [Bursa](https://www.bursa.ro/flanco-relanseaza-programul-de-afiliere-in-profitshare-23009046)
  - No current (2025–2026) Altex/Flanco commission data was found.
- **Compari.ro:**
  - Revenue model: merchants pay CPC (clicks only, not impressions). Base CPC is set by category and product price band; uncategorized clicks cost 0.3 RON — [Compari pricing](https://www.compari.ro/static/pricing-information.html)
  - New marketplace model: commission "from 6%" per one source vs 3–5.5% per another (conflict). Launch reported as July 2025 vs "2026" (also conflicting) — [Informateca](https://informateca.ro/compari-marketplace-romania-investitii-heureka/); [Business24](https://business24.ro/preturi-transparente/model-tranzactional-transparenta-preturi-1657728)
  - Romania was chosen as Heureka's first market for the integrated marketplace model — [ZF](https://www.zf.ro/business-hi-tech/sorin-adrian-dochian-country-manager-compari-platforma-comparare-23181804)
- **Shopmania:** rebranded in 2021 towards pre-purchase information and verified reviews. No current traffic or revenue data found — [Revista Biz](https://www.revistabiz.ro/shopmania-ro-trece-prin-rebranding-catre-o-noua-verticala-de-business/)

### Inferences
- Unit economics (illustrative, not sourced): at an average 3% on a 300 EUR ex-VAT basket (9 EUR/sale), 2,500 EUR/month needs ~280 approved sales/month. At a 2–3% click-to-sale conversion that is roughly 9,000–14,000 affiliate clicks/month, which implies tens of thousands of commercial-intent visits per month in Romanian.
- eMAG's 2-day window on electronics makes slow "research then buy later" content attribution weak. Email and YouTube audiences that click close to purchase fit better.
- The total ~7M EUR/year paid out by 2Performant to all its affiliates shows the ceiling: 2–3k EUR/month (24–36k EUR/yr) would be roughly 0.3–0.5% of all 2Performant affiliate payouts. That is achievable for a top affiliate but a high bar.
- Compari.ro and eMAG's own reach make generic price comparison a poor solo bet. Niche comparison in verticals eMAG covers weakly (insurance, energy, telecom, courses, B2B software) may fit better, but no commission data was found for them.

### Gaps
- Current eMAG category-by-category grid is behind the Profitshare login; not retrieved.
- No current Altex, Flanco, Dr. Max, Fashion Days, etc. commission data; no 2025–2026 data on the number of active affiliates or median affiliate earnings.
- No current data on Pricy.ro.

## 4. Display ads RPM: Romanian-language vs English traffic; ad network thresholds; Romanian ad networks

### Takeaway
Romanian traffic earns roughly 5–10x less per 1,000 views than US/English traffic, and premium managed networks are effectively closed to Romanian-language sites. Raptive requires 50% of traffic from US/UK/CA/AU/NZ. For a solo operator, display ads cannot realistically reach 2–3k EUR/month on Romanian traffic alone.

### Cited Findings
- **YouTube (2026 benchmark):** Romania creator RPM $0.71 (playback CPM $4.43) vs US RPM $5.14 — [Dynamoi](https://dynamoi.com/data/youtube-adsense-rpm)
- **AdSense, Romanian-language pages:** around $2 RPM in older research, on a page roughly 4 years old (dated) — [Partnerkin](https://partnerkin.com/en/blog/articles/adsense_rpm_rates_by_country)
- **US display RPM:** cited at roughly $15–40+ (blog benchmark) — [Techconda](https://www.techconda.com/2026/02/adsense-rpm-benchmarks.html)
- **Romanian news sites:** an average CPM of ~14 EUR was cited about 3 years ago. This likely reflects directly sold inventory on major outlets, not AdSense on small sites (dated, unclear) — [Actualitate.net](https://actualitate.net/a/cat-castiga-siturile-de-stiri-din-romania-din-publicitate)
- **Viewability:** a 2016 Gemius study found ~50% of display/video impressions on Romanian sites are never seen (old) — [iQads](https://www.iqads.ro/articol/36587/calitate-sau-cantitate-50-din-bugetele-de-display-video-cheltuite-pe-site-urile)
- **Advertiser-side benchmark:** a 2026 guide puts Google Display CPM in Romania at 5–20 RON (buy-side, not publisher revenue) — [DAFE](https://dafe.ro/en/blog/cpm-cpc-benchmark-romania)
- **Raptive:**
  - Minimum cut from 100,000 to 25,000 monthly pageviews in Oct 2025.
  - Sites with 25k–99,999 PV need ≥50% of traffic from US/UK/CA/AU/NZ; sites above 100k need ≥40%.
  - Primarily non-English or developing-market traffic may not qualify.
  - [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/raptive-drops-pageview-requirement-to-25-000-monthly-visits/); [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/raptive-drops-traffic-requirement-by-75-to-25000-views/558780/); [Hostragons](https://www.hostragons.com/ro/blog/conditii-aplicare-comparare-venituri-ezoic-mediavine/)
- **Journey by Mediavine:**
  - Accepts sites from ~1,000 monthly sessions with a 70% revenue share; the changes were effective 15 Jan 2026.
  - Sites are upgraded to full Mediavine at $5,000 ad revenue over a trailing 12 months.
  - Blog sources; verify with Mediavine — [Jupiter](https://www.jupiter.co/blog/mediavine-requirements-2026-how-to-qualify); [Productive Blogging](https://www.productiveblogging.com/?p=15446)
- **Ezoic:** no hard minimum (10,000+ pageviews recommended) and reports revenue as EPMV (earnings per 1,000 visitors) — [Clickio](https://blog.clickio.com/adsense-alternatives/)
- **AdSense pricing basis:** AdSense moved publisher payment from CPC to CPM (impression-based) — [Capital](https://www.capital.ro/google-adsense-model-plata-cpc-cpm.html)
- **CPM trend:** Raptive data (secondary) shows programmatic CPMs down ~12% YoY in Q4 2024 — [Live Breathe SEO](https://livebreatheseo.substack.com/p/finding-profitable-niches-in-2025)

### Inferences
- **Traffic needed for 2,500 EUR (~$2,900)/month from display (illustrative):**
  - Romanian traffic at a $1–2 page RPM: ~1.5–3M pageviews/month, i.e., top-tier Romanian publisher scale. Not viable solo.
  - English/US traffic at a $15–30 RPM on Raptive/Mediavine: ~100–200k pageviews/month. Viable in principle, but this is the segment hit hardest by AIOs, and the operator would compete globally.
- Display should be treated as a secondary monetization layer on Romanian sites (tools, databases), not the primary revenue model.

### Gaps
- No current, verifiable 2025–2026 AdSense/Ezoic page RPM figures for Romanian-language display traffic.
- No reliable list of Romanian publisher-side ad networks/programmatic sellers for small sites was found (searches returned only buy-side benchmarks).

## 5. Local lead-gen / rank-and-rent in Romania: niches, lead prices, platforms, Google LSA

### Takeaway
Romanian lead prices are an order of magnitude below US levels. Local service CPCs are ~1–6 lei for trades, ~3–12 lei for dentists and ~5–25 lei for lawyers, and agency-reported CPLs are ~10–20 EUR for typical service niches. Competing platforms (Daibau, Meserias Local, Caut-meserias, Recomand, CautAjutor) mostly sell cheap subscriptions (e.g., 49–199 RON/month) rather than per-lead fees. Google Local Services Ads (LSA) does not appear to be available in Romania. Rank-and-rent is undocumented in Romania.

### Cited Findings
- **Google Ads CPC in Romania (agency blogs):**
  - Local trades ("instalator"): 1–5 lei or 1–6 lei (avg ~3 lei).
  - Dentistry: 3–12 lei (or 3–8 lei for health/dental/cosmetics).
  - Legal: 4–12 lei, 8–25+ lei, or avg 12 lei in a 5–25 lei range.
  - National average search CPC ~2 lei; one Google forecast cited at 0.92 lei (~0.18 EUR).
  - US comparison: lawyers $8.58–9.87, dentists $7.85–8.00 per click.
  - [Carpathian Marketing](https://www.carpathian-marketing-agency.ro/insights/cat-costa-google-ads-romania); [Marva](https://marvamarketing.ro/cat-costa-google-ads-in-romania-in-2026-preturi-reale-si-factori-care-conteaza/); [Magnitude Web](https://magnitudeweb.ro/ghiduri/google-ads/cat-costa-google-ads-romania-2026); [DAFE](https://dafe.ro/en/blog/cat-costa-google-ads-romania); [Focus Digital](https://focus-digital.co/average-google-ads-cost-per-click-by-industry/); [PPC Chief](https://ppcchief.com/google-ads-cost)
- **Cost per lead in Romania (agency claims, not independent):**
  - Brand Developer: "normal" CPL 3–50 EUR depending on industry; 10–20 EUR for most service niches (cleaning, fit-outs, auto service, routine medical); 3–12 EUR auto; 5–20 EUR construction/renovation.
  - ACE agency: HoReCa 15–30 lei; real estate 50–150 lei; financial services 80–200 lei; B2B 100–300 lei.
  - A minimum workable ad budget of ~500 EUR/month per channel.
  - No official legal-sector CPL benchmark exists in Romania.
  - [Brand Developer](https://brand-developer.com/ro/blog/cost-pe-lead-normal-romania); [ACE Agency](https://aceagency.ro/servicii/lead-generation); [Brand Developer – budget](https://brand-developer.com/ro/blog/buget-minim-campanii-leaduri-romania); [Tucamaria](https://www.tucamaria.ro/cost-per-lead-in-avocatura-din-romania-benchmark-2026-date-verificate-per-canal/)
  - Lead quality example: 9,493 lei spent bought 143 leads (~66 lei/lead), and only 17% booked directly. CPL alone is misleading — [Story Seeker](https://storyseeker.ro/blog/noutati/poti-avea-cost-per-lead-mic-si-tot-sa-pierzi-bani/)
- **Daibau.ro (incumbent trades/renovation marketplace):**
  - Free for clients and funded by executant subscriptions, with no commission. Requests go to all registered firms in the city, and it works only with firms registered at ONRC.
  - Claims 6,000+ new requests/month and 1M+ visitors/year (self-reported). Subscription prices are not public.
  - [Daibau – how it works](https://www.daibau.ro/cum_functioneaza); [Daibau – executant signup](https://www.daibau.ro/inregistrare); [Digi24 (P)](https://www.digi24.ro/stiri/economie/consumatori/p-cauti-un-executant-pentru-lucrari-de-reparatii-renovare-sau-constructii-pe-daibau-ro-ii-gasesti-pe-toti-din-zona-in-care-stai-1354169)
- **Other Romanian platforms:**
  - Meserias Local: optional subscriptions for tradespeople, PRO 49 RON/month and BUSINESS 199 RON/month (in-app pricing) — [Meserias Local](https://www.meseriaslocal.ro/)
  - Caut-meserias.ro: first 5 requests free, then a subscription; no commission — [Caut-meserias](https://caut-meserias.ro/)
  - Recomand: no mandatory subscription, no commission — [Recomand](https://recomand.app/); [Agerpres](https://agerpres.ro/ots/recomand-platforma-care-schimba-modul-in-care-romanii-gasesc-un-meserias-descrii-lucrarea-o-singura---661409)
  - CautAjutor.ro: launched Sept 2026 with a free provider account; a "response" (credit) is consumed only when the client opens the offer — [StartupCafe](https://startupcafe.ro/cauti-un-instalator-s-a-mai-lansat-o-platforma-online-romaneasca-de-meseriasi-cautajutor-ro-107518)
  - Also present: Meseriasi.ro ("Platforma #1") and Mastero — [Meseriasi.ro](https://www.meseriasi.ro/); [Mastero](https://ajutor.mastero.ro/blog/o-platforma-sigura-pentru-mesteri-si-clienti/)
- **Google LSA:** the European launch (2020) covered 10 countries: AT, BE, FR, DE, IE, IT, NL, ES, CH and the UK. A later Google post lists home services in UK, FR, DE, ES, IT, IE, CH, BE and AT. Romania appears in neither list — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/?p=341284); [Google blog](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/a-new-way-for-legal-firms-and-estate-agents-to-reach-more-customers/)
- **Rank-and-rent:** general definition: rank a niche/city site, then rent the whole site or sell its leads to one local business. Practical pain points are call forwarding and call tracking. No Romanian-specific sources, prices or case studies were found — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/rank-and-rent/321315/); [SaveMyLeads](https://savemyleads.com/blog/other/rank-and-rent-local-lead-generation)
- **Local query behaviour:** the local pack shows on >90% of simple "service + city" queries, and AIOs on only ~15% of them — [Whitespark](https://whitespark.ca/blog/case-study-the-prevalence-of-ai-overviews-in-local-search/)

### Inferences
- **Price ceiling.** A Romanian plumber can buy a Google Ads click for ~0.2–1.2 EUR, so a raw call lead via ads costs roughly 2–12 EUR. That caps what trades will pay for a lead or a rented site, and platforms already sell unlimited requests for ~10–40 EUR/month.
- **Volume needed in trades.** For 2,500 EUR/month at ~10–15 EUR per lead, a solo operator would need ~170–250 sold leads/month, or ~10–25 rented sites at an assumed 100–250 EUR/month (assumed rent, not sourced).
- **Higher-value niches.** Lead value is likely higher (by ticket size; inference, not sourced) in:
  - dental implants / orthodontics, lawyers (divorce, succession, criminal), cadastru/intabulare
  - photovoltaic / heat-pump installs (Casa Verde programs), relocations/movers, HVAC (frigotehnisti), insurance/credit brokers
  - B2B services (accounting, ISO/fire-safety/SSM compliance), at agency-cited 100–300 lei per B2B lead
- **Best fit.** Exclusive per-lead sales to 1–3 buyers per city in high-ticket niches look more realistic than trades. Romanian SMBs' willingness to pay monthly retainers is unproven by any source found.
- **GDPR.** Reselling consumer contact data across multiple buyers is a GDPR issue (EEA); a lead form must disclose who receives the data (legal inference, verify with counsel).

### Gaps
- No published Romanian price list for exclusive leads by niche, and no Daibau subscription prices.
- No Romanian rank-and-rent rent levels or case studies.
- No current OLX Servicii pricing found.
- Official Google LSA country list for 2026 was not retrieved.

## 6. Paid directories / listing subscriptions as recurring revenue (examples and price points)

### Takeaway
Romanian niche directories exist with a free-listing plus paid-tier model. Price points are low: ~99 lei/year to ~1,800 lei/year typical, with premium tiers up to ~9,000 lei/year. Hitting 2–3k EUR/month needs on the order of 80–250 paying listings, which is feasible only in a niche with many fragmented, ad-spending vendors (weddings, events, trades, clinics, B2B suppliers).

### Cited Findings
- **Wedding directories:**
  - Nuntile.ro: free listing; paid plan 99 lei/year (priority in results plus couples' requests by email) — [Nuntile.ro packages](https://nuntile.ro/pachete/)
  - Wedline: PRO plan 249.99 RON/month, discounted to 149.99 RON/month (1,799.88 RON/year); ELITE tier 8,999.88 RON/year; START free — [Wedline](https://wedline.ro/companii)
  - SmartWedding: Premium 38 lei/month (down from 50 lei/month), 12-month minimum; the offer was dated "valid until 31.03.2026" — [SmartWedding](https://smartwedding.ro/furnizori)
- **Trades platforms (directory-like subscriptions):**
  - Meserias Local: 49 RON/month (PRO) and 199 RON/month (BUSINESS) — [Meserias Local](https://www.meseriaslocal.ro/)
  - Daibau: subscription-funded, prices not public — [Daibau](https://www.daibau.ro/cum_functioneaza)
- **Company data subscriptions:** Termene.ro (company data/monitoring, freemium plus paid) reported +81% turnover in a prior year and has considered a stock-market listing (dates 2020–2023; prices not found) — [Bursa](https://www.bursa.ro/termenero-crestere-de-81-procente-a-cifrei-de-afaceri-anul-trecut-47849637); [Economica](https://www.economica.net/?p=652374); [StartupCafe](https://startupcafe.ro/verificare-firme-termene-antreprenori-riscuri-htm-14078)
- **Global vendor claims (unverified, promotional):**
  - Tiered featured listings at e.g. $30/month (Bronze) to $100/month (Gold) — [Directorist](https://directorist.com/blog/online-directory-business-model/)
  - Niche directories with 500+ quality listings "average $2,100–4,500/month" (unnamed "industry data") — [The Elite Engines](https://theeliteengines.com/guides/make-money-with-a-directory-website.html)
  - 4–8 months to first paid-listing income — [aDirectory](https://adirectory.io/how-directory-websites-work/)

### Inferences
- **Subscriber count needed (~12,500 RON ≈ 2,500 EUR/month):**
  - at ~150 RON/month (Wedline PRO level): ~85 subscribers
  - at ~49 RON/month: ~255 subscribers
  - at 99 lei/year (Nuntile level): >1,500 subscribers, which is not viable
- **Directory sales are sales.** In Romania, recurring listing revenue depends on outbound selling (phone, WhatsApp) to SMBs. That is a non-AI, time-heavy component for a solo operator.
- **The strongest variant** combines a directory with data that vendors cannot easily replicate (verified prices, availability, reviews, regulatory status), plus lead routing. That is also the most AIO-defensible pSEO form (see §2).

### Gaps
- No Romanian B2B niche directory price points (e.g., industrial suppliers, HoReCa suppliers, clinics) found.
- No churn or retention data for Romanian listing subscriptions; Termene.ro / ListaFirme.ro current pricing not retrieved.

## 7. Website flipping: valuation multiples (Empire Flippers, Flippa, Motion Invest) 2025-2026

### Takeaway
Content-site multiples have compressed from the mid-30s× monthly profit (2019–2021) to the high-20s× by 2023–2024. In 2025, Flippa saw small deals average ~1.8–2.3x annual profit and content sites underperform. A site netting 2,500 EUR/month would realistically sell for roughly 55–85k EUR, before discounts for AIO risk and Romanian-language buyer scarcity.

### Cited Findings
- **Empire Flippers:** switched to annual multiples on 8 Sep 2026 ("nothing about our valuations or listing prices has changed"); example: 36x monthly = 3.0x annual — [Empire Flippers](https://empireflippers.com/switching-to-annual-multiples/)
  - Content-site multiples were ~27x monthly in 2024. Mid-2025 commentary focused on average sale price (~$325k across ~30 transactions) rather than multiples — [Empire Flippers](https://empireflippers.com/you-can-sell-your-business-for-life-changing-money-in-2025-heres-the-secret/)
  - A third-party guide cites EF guidance of ~1.7x–5x+ annual net profit (unverified) — [Earnifyhub](https://www.earnifyhub.com/blog/exit-strategy-online-businesses-valuation-guide)
- **Motion Invest:** yearly average multiples fell from 38.2x monthly (2019) to 28.4x (2023), with a historic range of 30–43x depending on size. No 2025 average was found — [Niche Pursuits](https://www.nichepursuits.com/?p=56963)
- **Flippa 2025 review:**
  - Content sites were down 33.5% in performance, while SaaS and YouTube rose.
  - Average profit multiples (annual) by deal size: 1.8x ($10K–100K), 2.1x ($100K–250K), 2.3x ($250K–1M), 2.9x ($1M+). Top-quartile ~3.9x at the smallest band and ~5.4x at the largest.
  - [Flippa 2025 insights](https://flippa.com/blog/2025-online-business-ma-insights-from-flippa/)
- **Flippa H1 2026 (marketplaces, not content):** median 2.02x annual profit, from 1.68x ($10K–100K) to 2.43x ($1M+) — [Flippa H1 2026](https://flippa.com/blog/the-new-era-of-digital-ma-flippas-h1-2026-insights-report/)
- **Other content-site benchmarks:**
  - Flippa's older content-market analysis: top-quartile content sites averaged 5.23x profit (date unclear) — [Flippa](https://flippa.com/blog/?p=44972)
  - Publisher guide (2026): standard content sites 28–42x monthly; authority sites with diversified monetization and owned audiences 45–60x+. This is a broker-adjacent source, optimistic — [Newor Media](https://newormedia.com/blog/?p=1519)
  - Buyers now stress-test valuations against "-20% organic traffic over two years" — [Newor Media](https://newormedia.com/blog/?p=1519)

### Inferences
- A 2,500 EUR/month (30k EUR/year) Romanian content/affiliate site would plausibly fetch ~1.8–2.8x annual, i.e., ~55–85k EUR.
- The buyer pool for Romanian-language sites on EF/Flippa/Motion Invest is thin, and AIO and core-update risk is priced in. A private sale to Romanian buyers or a local business is the more likely exit.
- Flipping is a weak "plan A" for a 2–3k EUR/month income goal. It works as an option on top of a cash-flowing asset.

### Gaps
- No data on sales of Romanian-language sites on any marketplace.
- No Motion Invest or EF 2025–2026 content-site multiple from primary pages (fetch blocked).

## 8. Realistic timelines to 2-3k EUR/month and failure rates

### Takeaway
Hard data says most new pages never reach the top 10 within a year (1.7–5.7%). Algorithm updates have wiped out a large share of niche sites (one study: 49% of 7,105 sites lost >90% of traffic Dec 2023–Aug 2024). A realistic expectation for a solo operator is 12–24+ months to 2–3k EUR/month, with a high failure probability for informational sites. Faster, lower-risk paths exist where revenue does not depend on mass Google traffic (selling leads or subscriptions to businesses).

### Cited Findings
- **Ahrefs ranking studies:**
  - Original study: only 5.7% of pages ranked in the top 10 for at least one keyword within a year. Most that did got there in ~2–6 months, and only 0.3% ranked top 10 for a high-volume keyword in under a year — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/?p=14254)
  - Newer figure: 1.74% of newly published pages reach the top 10 within the first year. Sample size is inconsistently reported (1M vs 2M pages), and secondary summaries say 40.82% of those that did rank got there in the first month — [Ahrefs (ES)](https://ahrefs.com/blog/es/how-long-does-it-take-to-rank/); [Whitehat SEO](https://whitehat-seo.co.uk/blog/how-long-does-it-take-to-rank-on-google)
- **Algorithm-update casualties:**
  - Paul Teitelman study of 7,105 niche sites (secondary): 49% lost >90% of monthly organic traffic between Dec 2023 and Aug 2024; ~22% lost all of it — [EarnifyHub](https://earnifyhub.com/blog/blogging/google-hcu-blogs-recovery-2026); [The Digital Bloom](https://thedigitalbloom.com/learn/2025-organic-traffic-crisis-analysis-report/)
  - Niche Site Growth, 11,500 Mediavine/Raptive sites through the Dec 2024 spam update: 13% had declines >20% and 5% gains >20% — [Niche Site Growth](https://nichesitegrowth.beehiiv.com/p/revealing-sites-well-helpful-content-update)
  - The Dec 2025 core update was described by an operator tracking ~200 affected sites as a shift "from content quality to content necessity" (anecdotal) — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/synergistdigitalmedia/googles-december-2025-helpful-content-update-hit-different-recovery-tactics-that-actually-work-3nl2)
- **Directories:** 4–8 months to first paid-listing income (vendor estimate) — [aDirectory](https://adirectory.io/how-directory-websites-work/)
- **Affiliate payouts:** a sale can take several stages before approval (cancellations and returns are reversed), so cash lags traffic. Profitshare says no income is guaranteed — [Maior Army](https://www.maiorarmy.ro/cum-poti-castiga-bani-online-promovand-produse-pe-care-oricum-le-recomanzi/); [2Performant commission statuses](https://support.2performant.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004102068-care-sunt-statusurile-comisioanelor-)
- **Commission cuts as an income risk:** Amazon Associates cut kitchen commissions to ~3% (secondary) — [Live Breathe SEO](https://livebreatheseo.substack.com/p/finding-profitable-niches-in-2025)

### Inferences
- **Timeline by model (synthesis; not from a single source):**

  | Model | Time to 2–3k EUR/month | Probability for an average solo operator |
  |---|---|---|
  | Romanian informational blog + affiliate/display | 18–36 months | Low (likely <10–20%), given Romanian RPMs and eMAG's short windows |
  | Romanian local lead-gen / niche directory with outbound sales | First revenue in 3–6 months; 2–3k EUR in ~9–18 months | Moderate, gated by selling ability, not SEO alone |
  | Data/tool pSEO site in Romanian | 6–18 months to meaningful traffic | Depends on genuinely unique data; display/affiliate income on Romanian traffic stays small unless paired with B2B leads or subscriptions |

- **Low capital fits; time and selling are the bottleneck.** The <2,000 EUR budget is enough: domains, hosting, a few data sources, call-tracking numbers. AI assistants lower build cost, but Google's scaled-content and core updates specifically penalize unedited AI output at volume, so build cost is no longer the bottleneck.
- **Portfolio approach.** Diversifying across 3–5 small properties or niches and building owned channels (email, Business Profiles, WhatsApp lists) reduces single-update risk. This is consistent with what buyers pay premiums for (§7).

### Gaps
- No survey with disclosed methodology on time-to-income or failure rates for niche sites in 2025–2026 (global or Romanian).
- The Teitelman 7,105-site study primary source was not retrieved.
- No Romanian operator income reports (transparent case studies) found.
