# Romanian legal, tax and payments framework 2026 for a solo online business earning 2,000-3,000 EUR/month

Research date: 2026-10-20. Method note: Firecrawl was out of credits and the sandbox proxy blocked direct page fetches of Romanian sites (startupcafe.ro, keez.ro, contapp.ro, bono.ro returned DNS/403), so findings come from web-search result extracts of the cited pages, not full-page reads. Almost all sources are Romanian accounting blogs, fintech blogs and news sites; very few are primary texts (Monitorul Oficial / ANAF). Where sources conflict this is flagged. The assignment brief's premise that the CASS cap rose to 72 minimum wages via Legea 141/2025 is corrected below: most sources attribute it to **Legea 239/2025**.

Key legal acts referenced across sources (for the report writer):
- **Legea 141/2025** (fiscal package 1, summer 2025): VAT 19% to 21% and 5%/9% to 11% from 1 Aug 2025; dividend tax 10% to 16% from 1 Jan 2026.
- **OG 22/2025**: VAT exemption threshold 300,000 to 395,000 lei from 1 Sep 2025; small-enterprise VAT changes.
- **Legea 239/2025** (fiscal package 2, in force 18 Dec 2025): PFA CASS cap 60 to 72 minimum wages (2026 income); SRL minimum share capital 500 / 5,000 lei.
- **OUG 89/2025** ("ordonanța trenuleț", Dec 2025): micro single rate 1% (3% removed); non-taxable part of minimum wage 300 to 200 lei from 1 Jul 2026; e-Factura deadline 5 working days; IMCA/ICAS and construction tax removed from 2027.
- **HG 146/2026**: minimum wage 4,050 to 4,325 lei from 1 Jul 2026.
- **OUG 6/2026, Legea 88/2026**: e-Factura for persons identified by CNP (transition to 1 Jun 2026, then optional).
- **OUG 8/2026**: 3% bonification on income tax in the Declarația Unică for 2025 income.
- **Ordin MF 1960/2025**: norma de venit nomenclature realigned to CAEN Rev. 3, for 2026 income.

## 1. PFA in 2026: sistem real vs norma de venit, income tax, CAS/CASS bases and caps, minimum wage, CAEN codes for norma

### Takeaway
For 2026 income a PFA pays 10% income tax, 25% CAS and 10% CASS. CAS has two fixed bases: 12 minimum wages (48,600 lei, so 12,150 lei) or 24 minimum wages (97,200 lei, so 24,300 lei). CASS is 10% of actual net income with a minimum base of 6 minimum wages (24,300 lei, so 2,430 lei) and a maximum of **72** minimum wages (291,600 lei, so 29,160 lei); the maximum was 60 for 2025. All thresholds use the **4,050 lei** minimum wage in force on 1 January 2026, even though the minimum wage rose to 4,325 lei on 1 July 2026. Norma de venit is effectively unavailable for software/IT (6202 and 6203 are excluded since 2024, and 6201 eligibility is disputed) and requires switching to sistem real once receipts pass 25,000 EUR. That rules it out for a 24,000-36,000 EUR/year online business.

### Cited Findings
**Rates and bases (2026 income):**
- CASS for independent activities is a flat 10% of net income, not tiered. The ceiling rose from 60 to 72 minimum salaries for 2026 income: 72 × 4,050 = 291,600 lei, so the maximum CASS is 29,160 lei/year. The minimum base is 6 salaries (24,300 lei), so the minimum CASS is 2,430 lei. The 6/12/24 tiers apply only to passive income (dividends, rent, interest). — [calculvenituri.ro](https://calculvenituri.ro/blog/cass-pfa-2026-plafoane-exemple); [contapp.ro Taxe PFA 2026](https://contapp.ro/blog/taxe-pfa-2026/); [avocatnet.ro](https://www.avocatnet.ro/articol_70992/Venituri-independente-2026-Baza-anual%C4%83-maxim%C4%83-de-calcul-la-CASS-egal%C4%83-cu-72-de-salarii-minime-nu-60.html)
- **Legal basis conflict:** most sources say the 60 to 72 cap increase was made by **Legea 239/2025** and applies to 2026 income. They also say neither Legea 141/2025 nor Legea 239/2025 changed the 10% CASS rate. — [avocatnet.ro](https://www.avocatnet.ro/articol_70992/Venituri-independente-2026-Baza-anual%C4%83-maxim%C4%83-de-calcul-la-CASS-egal%C4%83-cu-72-de-salarii-minime-nu-60.html); [contapp.ro Plafoane fiscale 2026](https://contapp.ro/blog/plafoane-fiscale-2026-pentru-pfa-ii-si-microintreprinderi/). A news piece framed it as a "fiscal bomb" for PFAs costing up to 4,860 lei more in CASS. — [dcbusiness.ro](https://www.dcbusiness.ro/bomba-fiscala-pentru-pfa-uri-a-intrat-in-vigoare-cine-poate-plati-cu-4-860-de-lei-mai-mult-la-cass_695501.html)
- CAS (pension, 25%) does not use the 72 cap. It is mandatory only if net income exceeds 12 minimum salaries (48,600 lei) and is optional below that. Base = 12 salaries (CAS 12,150 lei) if net income is between 12 and 24 salaries, or 24 salaries (CAS 24,300 lei) if net income is 24 salaries or more. — [calculvenituri.ro](https://calculvenituri.ro/blog/cass-pfa-2026-plafoane-exemple); [permisdeantreprenor.ro Taxe PFA 2026](https://permisdeantreprenor.ro/taxe-pfa-2026/); [storno.ro](https://storno.ro/ghid/taxe-pfa-2026)
- CAS and CASS are both deductible from the income-tax base: income tax = 10% × (net income − CAS − CASS). — [contabilitatepebune.ro](https://www.contabilitatepebune.ro/calculator-taxe-pfa-sistem-real/); [zarinacrm.ro](https://www.zarinacrm.ro/calculator-pfa-2026/)
- Worked example from search sources, 150,000 lei net income in sistem real: CAS 24,300 + CASS 15,000 + tax 11,070 (10% × 110,700) = 50,370 lei total (33.6%), leaving 99,630 lei. — [contapp.ro](https://contapp.ro/blog/taxe-pfa-2026/); [permisdeantreprenor.ro](https://permisdeantreprenor.ro/taxe-pfa-2026/)

**Which minimum wage applies:**
- The minimum gross wage was 4,050 lei from 1 Jan 2025 through 30 Jun 2026. HG 146/2026 (Monitorul Oficial, 13 Mar 2026) raised it to **4,325 lei from 1 Jul 2026** (+6.8%; 25.949 lei/hour). — [StartupCafe HG 146](https://startupcafe.ro/salariul-minim-2026-in-monitorul-oficial-hg-146-96219); [Economedia](https://economedia.ro/salariul-minim-creste-la-4-325-lei-de-la-1-iulie-cati-bani-primesc-angajatii-in-mana-si-cu-cat-cresc-costurile-firmelor-punctul-de-amenda-se-mareste-si-el.html)
- For CAS/CASS thresholds and bases, the minimum wage in force on **1 January of the income year** applies (4,050 lei for 2026). A mid-year increase does not change the thresholds. — [impozitul.ro/cass](https://impozitul.ro/cass); [StartupCafe expert explainer](https://startupcafe.ro/ce-salariu-minim-se-va-lua-in-calcul-la-stabilirea-plafoanelor-pentru-contributiile-din-2026-explicatiile-unui-expert-contabil-101726); [contapp.ro Plafoane 2026](https://contapp.ro/blog/plafoane-fiscale-2026-pentru-pfa-ii-si-microintreprinderi/)
- **Conflict:** one calculator uses post-July figures (24 MW = 103,800 lei; 72 MW = 311,400 lei, i.e. 4,325 lei), unlike the majority. — [regnet.ro calculator](https://www.regnet.ro/calculator-taxe-pfa-2026/)

**Norma de venit:**
- Ordin MF 1960/2025 (Monitorul Oficial nr. 1215, 30 Dec 2025) amends OMFP 925/2017 (the nomenclature of activities eligible for income norms). It realigns the list to CAEN Rev. 3, with each activity carrying its Rev. 2 and Rev. 3 codes, and applies from 2026 income. County ANAF directorates set the norm per position in the annex, and the CAEN codes listed are only examples. — [legislatie.just.ro Ordin 1960/2025](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/306193); [contapp.ro list CAEN Rev.3](https://contapp.ro/blog/lista-generala-de-activitati-impozitate-cu-norme-de-venit/); [ANAF Cluj norms 2026 PDF](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Cluj/AJFPSJ_norme_venit_2026.pdf)
- No norm can be lower than 12 minimum wages, so the minimum is 48,600 lei/year per activity in 2026. — [contzilla.ro](https://www.contzilla.ro/pfa-la-norma-de-venit-2026-venitul-net-anual/)
- CAEN **6202 and 6203** (IT consultancy, computer facilities management) cannot use norma de venit since 1 Jan 2024. According to Infotva, **6201** (software development) "was not included anyway". — [Infotva/manager.ro](https://infotva.manager.ro/articole/infotva/pfa-in-domeniul-it-atentie-la-codul-caen-ales-si-la-plafonul-de-tva-24079.html); [contzilla.ro](https://www.contzilla.ro/pfa-la-norma-de-venit-2026-venitul-net-anual/). **Contradicted by** a folositor.ro 2026 guide, which treats 6201 as eligible with a norm of about 50,000 lei/year varying by county (unverified). — [folositor.ro](https://folositor.ro/ghid/pfa-dezvoltator-software-2026). A draft order would remove position 120 (software creation) from the norma list; adoption status was not found. — [fiscalitatea.ro](https://www.fiscalitatea.ro/proiect-doua-coduri-caen-din-domeniul-it-ar-putea-trece-in-mod-obligatoriu-la-sistemul-real-de-impunere-23056/)
- 25,000 EUR rule: if receipts exceed 25,000 EUR in a year (converted at the BNR annual average; 2025 average 5.0415, so 126,037 lei), sistem real is mandatory from the next year. Receipts in 2026 above 25,000 EUR force sistem real from 2027. Foreign-currency receipts are converted at the BNR rate on the day received. — [contapp.ro Plafoane 2026](https://contapp.ro/blog/plafoane-fiscale-2026-pentru-pfa-ii-si-microintreprinderi/); [contapp.ro Plafoane 2025](https://contapp.ro/blog/plafoane-fiscale-2025-pentru-pfa-ii-si-microintreprinderi/)
- Under norma, income tax is 10% of the norm; CAS and CASS use the same 12/24 and 6–72 minimum-wage bases. The DU for 2026 income is due 25 May 2027. — [contapp.ro Taxe PFA 2026](https://contapp.ro/blog/taxe-pfa-2026/)

### Inferences
- **For this profile (24,000–36,000 EUR/yr, roughly 125,000–190,000 lei net), CAS is always at the 24-MW base (24,300 lei).** Net income passes 97,200 lei at roughly 19,000 EUR. CASS is 10% of net income (about 11,000–19,000 lei), well below the 29,160 lei cap. In practice the 72-MW cap change does not affect this profile; it only affects net incomes above 243,000 lei (about 47,000 EUR).
- Norma de venit is not a realistic route. Online services, SaaS and e-commerce codes are not shown as eligible in any source; 6202/6203 are excluded and 6201 is disputed. Even an eligible activity loses norma from the year after receipts pass 25,000 EUR, which a 2,000–3,000 EUR/month business crosses within 9–13 months.
- Worked norma example (illustration only, if an activity were eligible with a 48,600 lei norm): CAS 12,150 + CASS 4,860 + tax 10% × (48,600 − 12,150 − 4,860) = 3,159, total about 20,169 lei/yr. This assumes CAS/CASS are deductible under norma as in sistem real, which was **not verified**.
- An Întreprindere Individuală (II) is taxed like a PFA (same income tax, CAS, CASS). The only difference is the legal form (it can hire more employees). No 2026-specific source was retrieved for this, so treat it as unverified.

### Gaps
- Primary text of Legea 239/2025 / Codul fiscal art. 170 not read. The legal basis for 72 MW (239/2025 vs 141/2025) is per secondary sources only.
- Whether the minimum 6-MW CASS base still applies when the PFA owner also has a salary (exemption rules for persons insured through other income) was not confirmed for 2026.
- The full Ordin 1960/2025 annex was not retrieved. It is unconfirmed whether any CAEN Rev. 3 code for online retail (Rev. 2 4791), web portals (6312) or content creation can use norma in 2026, and whether position 120 (software) was removed.
- CAEN Rev. 3 recoding deadlines for existing PFAs/SRLs (ONRC) were not researched.

## 2. SRL micro-enterprise regime 2026, dividend tax, CASS on dividends, and net take-home comparison (PFA real vs SRL micro + dividends vs SRL micro + minimum salary)

### Takeaway
In 2026 a micro-enterprise pays a single **1%** tax on revenue (3% abolished). The revenue cap is **100,000 EUR** (509,850 lei at the 31 Dec 2025 rate), down from 250,000 EUR in 2025. It must have at least one full-time employee (or a director on a mandate contract paid at least the minimum wage). A shareholder with more than 25% stakes can keep only one micro company, and linked companies' revenues are aggregated. Dividends distributed from 1 Jan 2026 are taxed at **16%** (10% in 2025), plus CASS 10% on a fixed base of 6/12/24 minimum wages, capped at 9,720 lei. At 24,000–40,000 EUR revenue with low expenses, an SRL micro paying the owner the minimum wage and distributing the rest as dividends leaves about **4,500–8,500 lei/year more** than a PFA in sistem real, before the SRL's higher accounting/payroll cost of about 1,000–3,000 lei/yr. The advantage shrinks as revenue rises. An SRL on 16% profit tax (no employee) beats the PFA only at the low end (about 24,000 EUR) and loses at 30,000–40,000 EUR.

### Cited Findings
**Micro regime 2026:**
- Revenue cap: 100,000 EUR of the previous year's revenue for 2026, versus 250,000 EUR in 2025 and 500,000 EUR in 2024. This equals 509,850 lei at the BNR rate of 31 Dec 2025. Exceeding the cap during the year triggers 16% profit tax from the quarter in which it was exceeded. — [Keez blog](https://www.keez.ro/blog/din-2026-doar-firmele-cu-venituri-sub-100-000-euro-mai-pot-fi-micro-cum-te-afecteaza-noua-limitare-a-regimului-de-impozit-venit-microintreprindere.html); [money.ro](https://www.money.ro/stiri/microintreprinderi-2026-cota-de-1-si-plafonul-de-100000-euro-mrp1hoc8); [StartupCafe micro 2026](https://startupcafe.ro/microintreprinderea-in-2026-conditii-reguli-termene-anaf-93666)
- Single 1% rate from 1 Jan 2026. OUG 89/2025 removed the 3% rate, which in 2024–2025 applied to some CAEN codes and to revenue above 60,000 EUR. — [voilo.ro](https://voilo.ro/ro/blog/microintreprindere-2026-plafon-si-cota-de-impozitare/); [desteaptate.ro](https://desteaptate.ro/blog/microintreprindere-impozit-1-la-suta-2026); [Digi24 / ANAF conditions](https://www.digi24.ro/stiri/economie/anaf-a-anuntat-conditiile-pentru-microintreprinderi-in-2026-cine-plateste-impozit-de-1-si-cine-iese-din-sistem-3607245)
- The cap is applied to combined revenue of the company and its linked companies. One source says a >25% shareholder's PFA income is also counted. — [bono.ro micro](https://bono.ro/blog/impozit-micro-obligatii-fiscale-pentru-microintreprinderi); [bbconsulting.ro](https://bbconsulting.ro/impozitul-pe-veniturile-microintreprinderilor-in-2026-plafon-conditii-si-cota-de-impozitare/)
- At least one full-time employee is required, or an administrator on a mandate contract paid at least the gross minimum wage. New firms have 90 days to meet this. **Conflict** on the wage reference: 4,325 lei from 1 Jul 2026 per bono.ro, versus 4,050 lei per lightapp.ro. — [bono.ro](https://bono.ro/blog/impozit-micro-obligatii-fiscale-pentru-microintreprinderi); [lightapp.ro](https://lightapp.ro/blog/microintreprindere-2026.html); [alfasign.ro](https://alfasign.ro/ghid-microintreprinderi-2026/)

**Dividends 2026:**
- 16% for dividends **distributed** from 1 Jan 2026, withheld at source by the company. Dividends distributed by 31 Dec 2025 stay at 10% even if paid in 2026. — [permisdeantreprenor.ro](https://permisdeantreprenor.ro/impozit-pe-dividende-2026/); [calculvenituri.ro](https://calculvenituri.ro/blog/impozit-dividende-2026); [lawpedia.legal](https://lawpedia.legal/ro/06-fiscalitate/01-impozite/impozitare-dividende). - CASS on dividends: 10% of a fixed base of 6 MW (24,300 lei, so CASS 2,430), 12 MW (48,600 lei, so 4,860) or 24 MW (97,200 lei, so 9,720, the maximum), depending on total non-salary "other" income (dividends + rent + interest + investment gains). The 2026 bands use 4,050 lei despite the July increase. — [digigov.ro](https://digigov.ro/calculatoare/dividende); [calculvenituri.ro](https://calculvenituri.ro/blog/impozit-dividende-2026)
- **Conflict on CASS mechanics:** calculators disagree on whether gross or after-tax dividends are tested against the bands. One calculator wrongly uses 103,800-lei thresholds. — [digigov.ro](https://digigov.ro/calculatoare/dividende); [folositor.ro](https://folositor.ro/ghid/impozit-dividende-srl-2026); [estimez.ro](https://estimez.ro/calculator-dividende/)

**Minimum-wage salary (owner as employee), 2026:**
- H1 2026: gross 4,050 lei, with 300 lei of it non-taxable, gives net about 2,574 lei. **SRL formation (2026):**

### Inferences
**Net take-home model (my calculations from the cited 2026 rules).** Assumptions: 5.20 RON/EUR (close to the 2026 YTD average of 5.18; the spot rate is about 5.33 in Oct 2026); operating expenses 10% of revenue (tools, hosting, processor fees, accounting); CAS/CASS bases at 4,050 lei; no other income. Owner salary in the SRL micro case is the minimum wage on a full-time CIM all year: employer cost 6 × 4,134 + 6 × 4,418 = **51,312 lei**, owner net salary 6 × 2,574 + 6 × 2,699 = **31,638 lei**, so the state's share of salary is 19,674 lei. Dividends are taxed 16%, then CASS uses the 12-MW or 24-MW band (both gross and after-tax tests give the same band in these cases).

- Total tax wedge on profit before owner pay, at 30,000 EUR: PFA 48,546 lei (34.6%); SRL micro 40,098 lei (28.6%) including the salary's taxes.
- About 1,000–3,000 lei/yr must be subtracted from the SRL advantage for higher accounting and payroll costs (see Q7), plus formation costs and 500 lei of capital. Net advantage is about 5,000–7,500 lei/yr at 24,000–30,000 EUR and about 1,500–3,500 lei/yr at 40,000 EUR. The SRL also brings dividend timing flexibility and limited liability.
- **Pension trade-off:** the PFA pays CAS on a 97,200 lei base (24 MW). The SRL micro owner pays CAS on about 50,250 lei of salary (about 12.4 MW). The profit-tax SRL owner pays no CAS. Part of the SRL "saving" is lower pension accrual.
- "SRL micro + minimum salary" where profit is **left in the company** (no dividends) costs only the 1% plus the salary cost. Dividends can then be timed, with CASS capped at 9,720 lei/yr however large the distribution. This favours the SRL if the owner can live on the salary and distribute irregularly.
- The micro employee condition is the binding constraint. If the employee is a third party rather than the owner, the 51,312 lei is a pure cost (about 9,900 EUR/yr) and the SRL micro is worse than the PFA at this revenue.
- With EUR/RON at about 5.33 (Oct 2026), the same EUR revenue converts to about 2.5% more lei. The lei-fixed CAS/CASS bands do not move, so the PFA's CAS stays capped at 24,300 lei while income tax and CASS rise proportionally.

- Whether the 300/200-lei non-taxable minimum-wage facility applies to a shareholder-employee of their own SRL was not confirmed. Payroll figures use the general case.
- Mandate-contract (administrator) taxation was not modelled: no CIM facility, and CAM treatment not verified.
- The legal reserve, the annual D101/financial-statement costs, and the 2026 deadlines for micro D100 (quarterly) and dividend-tax D100 were not sourced.

## 3. VAT: registration threshold, rate, EU SME scheme, reverse charge, B2C digital services (10,000 EUR / OSS), art. 317, merchant of record treatment

### Takeaway
In 2026 the standard VAT rate is **21%** (since 1 Aug 2025; reduced rate 11%). The small-enterprise exemption threshold is **395,000 lei** turnover (since 1 Sep 2025, OG 22/2025), about 74,000–76,000 EUR at 2026 rates; it was 300,000 lei. VAT registration is due immediately on exceeding it, with no 10-day grace period. B2B services to foreign businesses (EU and non-EU) have their place of supply abroad and are **excluded** from the 395,000-lei count. B2C sales to EU consumers count. A non-VAT-registered PFA/SRL still needs a special VAT code under **art. 317** (form 700) before its first B2B service to an EU client or first purchase of EU/non-EU services. It then files D390 (recapitulative) for EU B2B sales and D301 for purchases on which it self-assesses 21% Romanian VAT without deduction. Above **10,000 EUR** of EU-wide B2C digital sales, the customer's country VAT applies. The options are to register for VAT in Romania and use OSS, use the EU cross-border SME exemption ("EX" suffix, EU turnover <100,000 EUR, available since 2025), or sell through a merchant of record. A merchant of record (MoR) turns all consumer sales into a single B2B sale to a foreign reseller.

### Cited Findings
- Exemption threshold 395,000 lei of calendar-year turnover, set by OG 22/2025 and in force from 1 Sep 2025 (previously 300,000 lei); remains valid in 2026. On exceeding it, the business must request registration that same day, and the invoice that breaks the threshold already carries 21% VAT. There is no longer a 10-day grace period. — [folositor.ro plafon TVA](https://folositor.ro/ghid/plafon-tva); [contapp.ro Plafoane 2026](https://contapp.ro/blog/plafoane-fiscale-2026-pentru-pfa-ii-si-microintreprinderi/); [Ministry of Finance press release](https://mfinante.gov.ro/en/acasa/-/asset_publisher/uwgr/content/suport-fiscal-pentru-c3-aentreprinderile-mici-prin-modific-c4-83ri-de-legisla-c8-9bie-cre-c8-99te-plafonul-de-scutire-de-tva-de-la-300.000-lei-la-395.000-lei); [Europa Liberă](https://romania.europalibera.org/a/plafon-tva-intreprinderi-mici-scazut/33515513.html)
- Standard rate 21% from 1 Aug 2025 (Legea 141/2025); reduced rate 11%. — [folositor.ro TVA 2026](https://folositor.ro/ghid/tva-romania-2026); [zarinacrm.ro](https://www.zarinacrm.ro/ghid-tva-firme-2026/)

Below it, a business may opt for destination taxation via OSS (binding for at least 2 calendar years). ### Inferences
- **At 2,000–3,000 EUR/month (about 125,000–190,000 lei/yr), a Romanian PFA/SRL is under the 395,000-lei threshold even if all revenue counted**, so it can stay a non-VAT payer. A business that sells mostly B2B/MoR abroad would not approach the threshold at all.
- **Selling SaaS/digital products directly to EU consumers** (own Stripe checkout): once EU B2C sales pass 10,000 EUR/yr (about 4–5 months at this revenue), customer-country VAT (17–27%) applies. Since the turnover is below 100,000 EUR EU-wide, the EX cross-border SME scheme may exempt sales in member states whose own national thresholds are not exceeded. Each state's threshold differs, so this requires per-country monitoring. The alternative is RO VAT registration plus OSS, which also means charging 21% to Romanian customers and filing VAT returns. Both add real compliance cost.
- **MoR route (Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, Gumroad, Stripe Managed Payments):** the MoR sells to the end customer and handles EU OSS, UK/US/other digital VAT/GST. The Romanian entity makes one B2B supply to a foreign reseller. If the MoR entity is non-EU (Lemon Squeezy and Gumroad are US companies; Paddle's reseller entity is believed to be UK-based, unverified), the place of supply is outside Romania. There is then no Romanian VAT and no D390, the turnover does not count toward 395,000 lei, and there is no e-Factura obligation (see Q4). If the MoR entity is in the EU, it is a reverse-charge B2B service (art. 317 code + D390 section P). For income tax, MoR payouts are ordinary business income (PFA) or revenue (SRL), recorded at the BNR rate on the day of receipt (PFA cash basis) or invoice date (SRL). Whether to book gross sales with the MoR fee as an expense, or the net payout as revenue, depends on the MoR contract (reseller vs agent) and should be decided with the accountant.
- **Hidden VAT cost for non-VAT payers:** purchases of foreign SaaS tools, hosting, ads (Google/Meta Ireland) and other services require self-assessing **21% Romanian VAT via D301 with no deduction**. This effectively adds 21% to foreign B2B service costs. Payment-processing fees may be VAT-exempt financial services, so possibly no D301 VAT on Stripe fees (unverified). Once the business is VAT-registered, this VAT becomes neutral.

### Gaps
- Primary Codul fiscal art. 310/316/317 texts were not read. The exact start date of the "place of supply abroad excluded from turnover" rule is in conflict (1 Sep 2025 vs 2026).
- Whether a non-VAT-registered Romanian business can register for the OSS Union scheme without art. 316 registration: secondary sources imply it cannot. Not confirmed by ANAF text.
- Stripe's acquisition of Lemon Squeezy (announced July 2024) was not confirmed in this session's results. Nor were FastSpring's terms or Paddle's exact contracting entity.
- No Romanian source was found on how ANAF treats MoR "self-billed" payout statements, or whether the PFA must still issue its own invoice to the MoR.

## 4. Invoicing compliance: RO e-Factura 2026 (B2B, B2C, PFAs, foreign clients), e-TVA, SPV, DAC7

### Takeaway
In 2026 every PFA/II/SRL identified by a CUI/CIF must send all domestic B2B and B2C invoices through RO e-Factura within **5 working days** (OUG 89/2025, from 1 Jan 2026; previously 5 calendar days). For B2C invoices without a CNP, the XML uses thirteen zeros. Persons identified only by **CNP** (e.g. copyright income) are exempt/optional from 1 June 2026 under **Legea 88/2026**, but PFAs invoicing under a CUI are not exempt. Invoices to foreign clients without a Romanian VAT code (EU B2B, non-EU, MoR) are outside mandatory e-Factura. Since 1 Jan 2026, invoices to foreign businesses that *are* VAT-registered in Romania are mandatory. Fines for PFAs run 1,000–2,500 lei. DAC7 is reported by platforms (form F7000 by 31 January), not by sellers.

### Cited Findings
- Transmission deadline is 5 working days from 1 Jan 2026 (OUG 89/2025), replacing 5 calendar days. — [permisdeantreprenor.ro](https://permisdeantreprenor.ro/e-factura-2026-termen-5-zile-lucratoare/)
- Persons identified by CNP: form 082 registration by 15 Jan 2026 for ongoing activity; OUG 6/2026 created a transition to 1 Jun 2026. **Legea 88/2026** (Monitorul Oficial nr. 459, 29 May 2026, effective 1 Jun 2026) makes e-Factura **optional** for CNP-identified persons. They can opt in via form 081 or request removal from the registers. — [facturi-online.ro OUG 6/2026](https://facturi-online.ro/e-factura-cnp-persoane-fizice-ou6-2026/); [StartupCafe Legea 88/2026](https://startupcafe.ro/e-factura-la-drepturi-de-autor-persoanele-fizice-care-lucreaza-pe-cnp-scapa-de-obligatie-presedintele-a-promulgat-legea-100573); [regnet.ro](https://www.regnet.ro/e-factura-pfa-2026-cine-e-obligat-dupa-legea-88-2026/); [infobuz.ro](https://www.infobuz.ro/stiri-de-actualitate/economie-finante/ro-e-factura-cnp-optionala-radiere-registru-2026/)

A PFA that also earns copyright income on CNP is in a dual position: PFA invoices are in e-Factura, copyright receipts are not. — [regnet.ro](https://www.regnet.ro/e-factura-pfa-2026-cine-e-obligat-dupa-legea-88-2026/); [calculvenituri.ro](https://calculvenituri.ro/blog/e-factura-drepturi-de-autor-2026)
- B2C: a PFA invoicing a private client is obligated (the B2C obligation dates from 1 Jan 2025). When the customer gives no CNP, the field takes 13 zeros; entering a fictitious CNP is warned against. — [contapp.ro e-Factura pe CNP](https://contapp.ro/blog/e-factura-pe-cnp/); [crmflow.ro](https://crmflow.ro/e-factura-pfa); [finans.ro](https://finans.ro/blog/e-factura-legea-88-2026-schimbari-b2c)

- Foreign clients: from 1 Jan 2026, invoices to non-established taxable persons **registered for VAT in Romania** must go through e-Factura (OUG 89/2025); the trigger is the client's Romanian VAT code. Otherwise, invoices for services with place of supply abroad (EU B2B with a valid VIES code, non-EU clients) are not mandatory. Intra-EU B2C depends on OSS. Whether voluntary submission is allowed is not clearly stated. — [factureaza.ro](https://blog.factureaza.ro/facturile-externe-ro-e-factura/); [enlivy.ro](https://enlivy.ro/ghid-efactura-anaf/); [regnet.ro clienți străini](https://www.regnet.ro/pfa-cu-clienti-straini-facturare-valuta-si-impozite-2026/); older: [easybill.ro](https://easybill.ro/ajutor/facturi-emise-catre-extern-raportare-anaf-e-factura)
- DAC7: transposed by O.G. 16/2023; form F7000 (OPANAF 1.996/2023). Filed annually by **platform operators** by 31 January for the previous year (first filing 31 Jan 2024 for 2023 data). Reports include seller identity, bank account, total consideration and number of activities; for rentals, also property address and days rented. ### Inferences
- Practical setup: register in SPV (ANAF online portal) with the PFA/SRL's CUI and use invoicing software that auto-submits to e-Factura (SmartBill, Oblio, FGO, Keez, etc.). Send every Romanian B2B/B2C invoice within 5 working days. Foreign invoices (EU B2B reverse charge, US/UK clients, MoR payouts) are issued normally (bilingual, EUR/USD allowed, BNR rate noted) but not sent to e-Factura unless the client has a Romanian VAT code.
- For a Stripe-checkout business with many Romanian consumers, every B2C sale needs an e-Factura invoice (13-zero CNP if none is given). That is an operational argument for an MoR or for automated invoicing integrations.
- DAC7 means income earned via Etsy, Upwork, Airbnb, app stores and similar platforms is visible to ANAF; it should always be declared.

### Gaps
- RO e-TVA (pre-filled VAT return) was not researched. It is relevant only after VAT registration (art. 316), so it is of low relevance to a non-VAT-payer at this revenue.
- The DAC7 de minimis thresholds (EU directive: sellers of goods with <30 sales and ≤2,000 EUR are excluded) were not confirmed in Romanian sources this session.
- Whether a B2C sale to an EU consumer (place of supply Romania, under 10,000 EUR) needs e-Factura with 13 zeros was not explicitly confirmed.

## 5. Affiliate and ad revenue from foreign companies (AdSense, Amazon Associates, YouTube): tax treatment, W-8BEN, withholding, reverse charge

### Takeaway
Ad and affiliate income from foreign companies is ordinary business income for a PFA/SRL. It is invoiced to the paying platform entity based on its payment report and declared in the DU (PFA) or company accounts. Payments from EU entities (e.g. Google Ireland) are B2B services under reverse charge (art. 317 code + D390); payments from US entities are outside the scope of Romanian VAT. YouTube applies US withholding only on earnings from US viewers: up to 24% without tax info, about 10% under the US–Romania treaty with a W-8BEN (older source). The US tax withheld can be credited against Romanian tax.

### Cited Findings
- US withholding on YouTube applies to non-US creators, only on US-viewer earnings. Without a tax form on file, YouTube withholds 24%; under the US–Romania treaty the rate can fall to about 10% by claiming treaty benefits on W-8BEN with a Romanian tax ID (CNP for an individual). (Source dated 2021.) — [Alexa&Partners](https://www.alexapartners.ro/2021/04/09/impozitare-venituri-youtube-s-u-a-pentru-creatorii-romani/); [Alexa&Partners 2](https://www.alexapartners.ro/2021/04/19/taxe-impozite-declarare-venituri-youtube-monetizare-legala-pe-youtube/)
- Google may require a Romanian tax residency certificate from ANAF before reducing withholding. — [arenafinanciara.ro](https://www.arenafinanciara.ro/venituri-din-youtube/)
- 2026 guide: a PFA invoices platform income to the platform based on the payment report. AdSense ad revenue maps to CAEN 7312, and income is declared via the Declarația Unică. — [solo.ro creators PFA 2026](https://www.solo.ro/blog/pfa-pentru-creatori-de-continut)
- Alternative without PFA (forum discussion, contested): declare YouTube income as copyright income with a 40% flat deduction and 10% tax on the remaining 60%, crediting the US withholding. Forum participants disagree on whether AdSense can be declared without a PFA. — [avocatnet.ro forum](https://www.avocatnet.ro/forum/discutie_873786?title=%E2%80%9EDeclararea-veniturilor-din-YouTube-AdSense-ca-persoan%C4%83-fizic%C4%83---Drepturi-de-autor-sau-alte-venituri)
- Persons identified by CNP earning copyright income no longer have a general e-Factura obligation from 1 Jun 2026. — [calculvenituri.ro](https://calculvenituri.ro/blog/e-factura-drepturi-de-autor-2026)

### Inferences
- VAT: AdSense/YouTube for EEA publishers is paid by a Google entity in Ireland (implied by the Irish DAC route), so it is a B2B service to an EU taxable person. A non-VAT-registered PFA/SRL needs an art. 317 code, issues invoices without VAT marked "taxare inversă", and reports in D390 section P. Amazon Associates commissions are reverse charge if the contracting Amazon entity is EU (e.g. Luxembourg), and outside the scope of Romanian VAT if US. In both cases the turnover is excluded from the 395,000-lei count. Neither entity is verified.
- W-8BEN is the individual form (PFA owner). An SRL would file **W-8BEN-E**. Website AdSense and affiliate commissions are generally service income not subject to US withholding. US-source YouTube royalties are subject to it, so treaty claims matter mainly for YouTube US views.
- Foreign tax credit: US tax withheld can be credited in the DU (PFA) up to the Romanian tax on that income, with a withholding certificate (Form 1042-S). For an SRL micro, crediting foreign tax against the 1% revenue tax is limited.

### Gaps
- The current IRS/Google withholding rates (24% backup vs 30% statutory) and the exact Romania–US treaty royalty rate (sources say about 10%; the treaty distinguishes cultural vs industrial royalties) were not verified against IRS primary sources.
- The contracting entities and invoicing mechanics for AdSense, YouTube and Amazon Associates for Romanian accounts (self-billing vs own invoice) were not confirmed from official docs.
- CAEN Rev. 3 code equivalents for content creation/affiliate marketing were not researched.

## 6. Payments: Stripe, PayPal, Revolut Business, Wise, Romanian processors (Netopia, EuPlătesc, PayU), fees, receiving USD/EUR, bank requirements

## 7. Declarația Unică deadlines and penalties; accountant costs PFA vs SRL in 2026

### Takeaway
The Declarația Unică (D212) for 2025 income was due **25 May 2026**. For 2026 income it is due **25 May 2027**, together with payment of income tax, CAS and CASS (PFA, and dividend CASS for SRL owners). A 3% bonification on income tax was granted for 2025 income if the DU was filed and everything was paid in full by 15 April 2026 (OUG 8/2026). It is not yet known whether it will repeat for 2026 income. Late filing fines are 50–500 lei (one source: 500–1,000 lei after 30 days), plus late-payment penalties of 0.01%/day. Indicative accountant fees: PFA in sistem real 160–500 lei/month plus 190–500 lei for the DU; small SRL 250–500 lei/month plus about 30 lei/employee/month payroll.

### Inferences
- Annual compliance cost estimate from the brig.ro ranges: PFA sistem real about 2,100–6,500 lei/yr (about 400–1,250 EUR), i.e. 12 × 160–500 + DU 190–500. SRL micro with one employee about 3,400–6,400 lei/yr (about 650–1,250 EUR), i.e. 12 × 250–500 + payroll. On top of that come items a PFA does not have or has smaller: annual financial statements (often billed extra), business bank account fees, ONRC filings for dividends/changes, and the qualified e-signature. That gives the estimated SRL premium of about 1,000–3,000 lei/yr used in Q2. This premium is my estimate, not a sourced figure.
- PFA cash-flow: since 2023 the PFA in sistem real pays tax, CAS and CASS once a year (by 25 May of N+1), so roughly 30–35% of net income should be reserved in a separate account through the year. An SRL micro pays the 1% quarterly, payroll monthly, dividend tax by the 25th of the month after payout, and dividend CASS via the DU by 25 May N+1.

- No independent survey of accountant prices was found (single source); prices vary between Bucharest and the provinces.
- Whether a DU bonification will be offered for 2026 income (paid in 2027) is unknown.

## 8. 2026-specific changes, 2025 vs 2026 comparison, and proposed 2027 measures (Bolojan government)

### Takeaway
The Bolojan government's 2025 austerity packages (Legea 141/2025, OG 22/2025, Legea 239/2025, OUG 89/2025) raised VAT to 21%, dividend tax to 16% and the PFA CASS cap to 72 MW. They cut the micro cap to 100,000 EUR and unified the micro rate at 1%. They raised the VAT exemption threshold to 395,000 lei and introduced 500/5,000-lei minimum SRL capital, 5-working-day e-Factura and a reduced minimum-wage tax facility. 2026 brought a minimum-wage rise to 4,325 lei (1 Jul), e-Factura relief for CNP-identified persons (Legea 88/2026) and a one-off 3% DU bonification. Confirmed for 2027: removal of IMCA/ICAS (minimum turnover taxes for large firms) and the construction tax. No specific 2027 proposals affecting PFAs, micro companies or dividends were found.

### Inferences
- Direction of travel: each 2025–2026 change narrowed the gap between PFA and SRL. The micro cap fell, dividend tax rose from 10% to 16% and the 3% band was removed. Higher CAS/CASS-like costs on dividends (CASS up to 9,720 lei) remain. For a 24,000–40,000 EUR solo business the SRL micro is still slightly ahead, but only because of the owner-as-minimum-wage-employee structure. The margin (about 1,500–7,500 lei/yr after costs) is small enough that a further dividend or micro change in 2027 could flip it.
- The leu's sharp weakening (5.09 to about 5.33 EUR/RON during 2026) raises the RON value of EUR revenue. It makes the 395,000-lei VAT threshold smaller in EUR terms (about 74,000 EUR at 5.33). The micro 100,000 EUR cap for 2027 will be converted at the 31 Dec 2026 rate.
- Anyone setting up now (Oct 2026) should be aware that any SRL created now needs 500 lei capital; the 2026 micro rules apply for the rest of the year; and 2026 PFA income is declared and paid by 25 May 2027 with CAS/CASS at the 4,050-lei bases.

### Gaps
- No official 2027 budget or fiscal-code draft affecting PFAs, micro companies, dividends or CASS was found as of 20 Oct 2026. The 2027 minimum wage and the minimum wage that will apply to 2027 contribution thresholds are unknown.
- The primary texts of Legea 141/2025, Legea 239/2025, OUG 89/2025, OUG 8/2026 and Legea 88/2026 were not read directly. All attributions are via secondary sources.
